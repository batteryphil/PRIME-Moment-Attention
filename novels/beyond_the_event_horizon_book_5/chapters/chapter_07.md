# Chapter 7: Railgun Salvos and Plasma Storms

The alerts screeched like tortured beasts as if to mirror his panic-racing heart. Leo slapped palms to temples, trying to block out irrelevant noise cluttering his sensitive ears. In this cramped, tin-can cockpit jacked up on max adrenaline, such petty luxuries meant nothing - it was senses only now, pure instinct.

He whipped around to scan the consoles where Vex worked the delicate dances of their systems under duress from forces nobody truly understood yet. The vortex twisted metal groaned in protest, starboard bulkheads warping ominously near failure from heat alone. They were running out of options fast.

"You seeing these readings? We're taking hits!" Leo bellowed, not caring about politeness anymore now that every second counted more than their lives.

"Yeah, course I am!" Vex retorted tersely back without ever breaking eye-contact with the screens. She chewed on her lower lip in concentration, but beneath the surface stoicism, fear gnawed at her like a predator ratting in sewers below.

"It's railguns! Multiple salvoes incoming!" Vex reported grimly, features shifting to pure focus. The sleek lines of asteroid mining freighters - or whoever the bastards were behind this ambush - resolved on targeting overlays as they lined up for kill shots.

"Gonna be fun dodging those slugs moving outbound in freefall...in this chaos." Leo spat bitterly. The mere thought of engaging maneuver thrusters too early risked throwing their fragile orbit off-kilter into interstellar meat-grinder velocities.

Suddenly, the void outside turned daybreak-bright as successive sonic booms lit up in flaring shockwaves of plasma discharges detonated along armor plating - each one a silent, ear-numbing concussion shuddering through bones. Sulfuric sparks rained down onto the command room deck plates like shooting stars gone awry in reverse. 

Leo's breath caught in his throat as awareness dawned of how incredibly vulnerable they were in close quarters. Every scrap of protective shielding would vaporize, turning them to ash long before any attacker even drew a bead.

"We can't let our guard down for a single second!" Vex roared, eyes blazing as she fed afterburner thrusts, spewing blue flames to try and gain separation speed despite knowing it might only give them split-second advantages. With railgun rounds chasing hot on plasma trails, escape seemed as elusive as capturing photons and bottling fairy dust.

Leo gritted teeth until jaw cracked in two. Time was running out - they needed something, anything to swing momentum their way...before everything went incandescent white light.

Leo watched anxiously from the copilot's seat as Astrid weaved the stolen corvette between incoming railgun slugs, every near-miss jolting him against the harness straps. The starboard viewport fractured into a spiderweb pattern as a stray shot punched through armored skin, atmosphere hissing away in a geyser of mist. Shrapnel peppered nearby consoles, sending sparks dancing across circuit boards. 

"We dodged those bastard slugs by mere meters," Vex panted, her voice clipped in focus despite the harrowing proximity. "But we can't ride this bucking bronco forever. This chaos needs an anchor, a plan—"

"A plan?" Leo snapped incredulously over the constant percussion of impacts and the acrid tang of ignited wiring, singeing hair at his nape. He swiped sweat from his brow, smearing grime that had dried on his forehead during their precarious evasive maneuvers. "When your own crew nearly blew us up? And now these pirates are raining hellfire! What's the damn strategy, huh?"

In truth, adrenaline-fueled tunnel vision numbed him to higher-level thinking. All he could grasp were nanoseconds of reaction-time geometry, a desperate bid to extend their survivable arc till some shred of salvation became reachable. But Vex had always thrived in this crucible while the mere possibility squeezed his gut into a vise. "We both know this is about trust, not tactics," he growled, jaw clenched tight enough to crack another tooth under unbearable stress. "Yours, mine—and the lives entrusted to our judgment."

The captain jerked in her seat as if slapped, icy blue gaze lancing back at him with searing intensity, betraying none of that emotion despite the ragged rhythm of her breathing and the tremble in fingers poised restlessly over firing grips. Her platinum dreadlocks whipped a vortex around her angular features, framing piercingly black irises flashing with a storm surge of unbridled fury and wild, savage intelligence. 

"Trust?" Her voice dropped to a husky purr edged with volcanic malice as she aimed a sardonic smirk in his direction before refocusing straight ahead, razor precision tracking threats crawling into range across all sensor feeds. "What planet did you crawl out from under—the one still clinging to outdated notions of 'honor'? In my world, it's sink or swim, bastard or boss. Survival's not a battle for chivalrous ideals—it's dog-eat-dog, tooth and claw. My crew, they're family—blood, bone and bitter loyalty forged in the fires of Hell itself. You want to be part of the tribe? Then stop acting like a relic from yesterday's shooting wars!"

Leo felt the temperature rising in his face, pulse pounding furious in his ears at her scathing characterization, but stony resignation tempered the sting. Vex spoke for a reason, one he begrudgingly acknowledged now—that reality was as harsh as the environments they operated in. When he'd first materialized in this strange, unforgiving future, there had been no time for the luxury of retooling centuries-worth of rigid moral boundaries. Each crisis demanded swift adaptation or death.

"So what do I need to do?" he bit out through grinding teeth, surrendering inch by agonizing inch to this new norm. "Cut loose the old man inhibitions, play your pirate way. And don't sugarcoat it, Captain—" His gaze locked intensely with hers, daring and anger simmering beneath the steely surface, willing her to understand— "that means embracing whatever dark corners in me will help keep the lights on."

✦ ✦ ✦

The freighter's hull shuddered violently as a chain of railgun slugs punched crater-sized holes through its flimsy bulkhead, sending twisted molten metal raining down in burning cascades. Cursing viciously, Leo lunged aside just as another brutal impact sent consoles and components flying. Scadding heat washed over him as circuitry exploded into open flames. His heart hammered against his ribs, lungs searing with each desperate gasp. Sweat and hydraulic fluid mingled on his skin as the fight-or-flight surge coursed through his veins.

Beside him, Captain Vex bellowed orders at her marauders, her face an impassive mask despite the cacophony of alarm klaxons shrieking through the cramped bridge. Flickering screens displayed fragmented status updates and target locks in a chaotic blur of red warnings and amber alerts as she coaxed maximum power out of the battered ship.

"Hold position! Shift our momentum, take them broadside so the guns can bear, boys and girls!" Her voice cut like a laser through the bedlam, firm yet urgent as she scanned the damage assessment. One ravaged eye caught Leo's, an unspoken acknowledgement passing between them - the desperate ballet of evasive maneuvers and salvo retaliation wouldn't last long. They needed decisive action or risk being torn apart hammer and tongs in this suicidal duel of heavy ordinance.

A scream tore through the howling wind outside and Vex's profile stiffened in grim recognition. "Damn," she growled low and deadly, eyes widening slightly. She shot a curt glance at Leo as she reached for the ship-wide comms key. "Mercer, you hear that? Our shielding's down, hull breaches compromised - we've got atmosphere hemorrhaging into space. Get up to engineering, patch those leaks and seal us off before we depressurize!"

"No bloody chance!" Despite the chaos around him, Leo spun to confront Vex with raw defiance etched on every angle of his rugged features. "I'm staying put. We're down to our last few seconds - either we finish taking down that freighter or turn tail and abandon ship."

"I'm the goddamn captain," Vex snarled back, incredulous and infuriated. "This is my call -"

"The hell it is!" Leo intercut brutally, grabbing her nearest hand with bruising force and yanking her toward the aft command console. "Not when lives are in peril and this bucket of bolts threatens to blow anyway. If you've got any use left in you beyond getting us captured or incinerated, start doing something about it! Now!"

In the frozen instant their gazes clashed, an electrical spark passed between them - hot, intense, charged with adrenaline and urgency - and in that heartbeat, roles reversed without either realizing it, each seeing the desperate determination reflected back at them. Vex's resolve crumbled, a tremor running through her fingers as they relaxed within Leo's grasp, acknowledging defeat...or perhaps victory.

With a ragged breath, the corsair captain nodded once, curt as a military salute. "Alright, Mercer. You've made your point." She leaned forward, blue-flecked eyes locking onto his again as she worked the console with feverish intensity. "Let's end this before we become part of it."

✦ ✦ ✦

Twin railgun bursts seared past, a hair's breadth from their straining hull-plating. Amidst the cacophonous shriek of accelerated slugs carving hypersonic arcs through relativistic exhaust plumes, Leo and Vex exhaled jointly in relief...before exhaling again in unison at the revelation unfolding around them on long-range sensors. They gazed bug-eyed at the data streaming across the navigation display - impossible coordinates for any known planet or station, but unmistakably celestial features shimmering against the starry black.

"What in the abyss..." Vex breathed aloud, even as the Starlight Marauder herself seemed to sense the anomaly. Thrusters reengaged instinctively, not away from danger—though that lurked mere kilometers behind—but inexorably toward these strange apparitions lurking unseen until now amidst the chaos.

"I don't know." The uncertainty in Leo's voice was a stark contrast to his authoritative tone earlier. He had faced many surreal environments since becoming lost in future spacetime, but nothing quite prepared him for the awe he felt beholding countless glittering rings stretching across light-years like celestial rainbows woven by some cosmic artist.

Riding gravity waves radiating from monstrous gas giants orbiting within those haloes, the Marauder surmounted barriers of distance and velocity, accelerating toward the heart of a nebula unlike anything humanity ever encountered. Interstellar clouds glowing purple to crimson hues crackled with energetic plasma, stellar embryos coalesced into blazing protostars...

Inexplicably drawn onward, both commander and captain found themselves hypnotized by the raw energy bathing their survey scopes—the pulsations, the swirls, hints of quantum foam breaking on the cosmic waves at the universe's farthest reaches. It took a wrenching jolt as shields flared to deflect a barrage of errant ionizable particles whipping up the nebular turmoil for either to break the mesmerizing trance.

"Holy f---," Vex managed to stammer out, rubbing both eyes before scrutinizing the readouts afresh. "Is that...a wormhole?"

Leo peered over her shoulder at the readings, lips parted in shock. Even armed with modern physics he struggled to fully comprehend the implications.

"The scaling suggests it could be stable enough for traversal," he mused slowly, "but such an opening would require massive gravitational wells on both ends..."

Their voices trailed off as a new, ominous warning klaxon pierced the tense stillness. Vex flashed Leo a frightened glance at reading indicating an imminent collision course—all attempts to correct their trajectory metamental resistance from whatever mysterious forces ensnared them in their relentless push toward the accreting behemoths.

"Incoming object," announced AI with detachment, "designated Object Prime Alpha-HL47, predicted intersection in twelve point five minutes..."
