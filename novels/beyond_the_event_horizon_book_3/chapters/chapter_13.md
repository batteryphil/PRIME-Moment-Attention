# Chapter 13: Armor Pierced

The sleek silvery hull of the Starlight Marauder reeled against its inertial dampeners as another cruel impact rocked it from stem to stern. Astrid Vex cursed hotly in two languages as warning lights erupted across her command helm like fireworks in the night sky. Even over the urgent din of claxons and strident voices, she could feel the telltale drop in main power, the tremble that bespoke the loss of radiator surface area.

"Hole incoming portside twenty meters!" bellowed her helmsman Rannick from the co-pilot station beside her. "Radiator strut took a direct hit!"

With a scowl, Astrid shifted gears to Manual Control and wrenched her helm down, sending the crippled corvette plummeting into the shadowed depths beyond Neptune's icy rings like a moth diving into flame. Leo Mercer clung wildly to his acceleration couch, watching in horror as stars swirled past in mad dance, their harsh radiance glinting off splattered fragments of ablative armor drifting back into the void. The screaming in his ears was abruptly replaced with the sickening hiss of decompressing compartments, followed swiftly by the acrid reek of ozone and scorched metal drifting up from exposed conduits beneath his feet.

They had evaded the initial onslaught of laser strikes, but at a terrible cost - his beloved ship was shedding structure with each passing second under relentless barrage from those monstrous Kraken-class guns. The shock waves of near-misses rattled teeth, while the eerie, surreal darkness of open vacuum mocked any notion he once held of Earth's protective bubble. As the battered vessel plowed onward deeper into the cold embrace of the Kuiper Belt, icy particles whizzed like daggers past bulkhead hatchways, threatening with every collision to rip the very heart out of their hull.

Astrid spared him one fiery glance before turning her attention back to the flickering tactical display. Sirens continued their unrelenting shriek even after they'd passed below the visual threshold, painting the interior in strobing bursts of crimson and gold. "Well shit," she muttered grimly, "looks like we blew our cover pretty damn thoroughly there. Guess we can skip this whole sneak attack thingy and go straight to guns blazing instead..."

Leo's lungs burned from shouting over the incessant wail of alarms still shrieking into damaged comms systems. He squinted against glare from the cracked viewport as bits of shattered asteroid crumbled loose from their pursuers' salvoes, each fragment a potential micrometeorite seeking their hides. His stomach pitched at every violent jolt shaking the ship's frame—the Starlight Marauder reeled within the thrumming grasp of the enemy star-guns. Sweat trickling down his cheek, stinging from the salt of adrenaline, Leo barked into the com—

"—what the fuck, Vex?!?"—

Astrid swung around just then, emerald eyes flashing a warning as a particularly vicious tremor slammed them both hard into the console. Mangled sensors crackled feebly back to life amidst the haze of wreckage, confirming what Leo already suspected with growing dread: those monsters had homed in tighter now, and were pressing their advantage relentlessly.

Vex snarled a curt reply, fingers dancing across the control board as she worked the ship's defenses without apparent pause. "Our options just got real limited, Mercer! Guess it's fight or die tonight." She tossed him a venomous sidelong glance that sent a chill racing down his spine. "So what's your move here, huh? You think you're still gonna play hero and take point?"

Leo clenched his jaw, steeling himself against the bitter taste of defeat. They were beaten, outgunned, outnumbered—and yet the sheer fury burning in his gut would not be quenched. "We didn't train for this," he growled, conviction hardening his voice, "we prepared for any goddamn scenario. There's a way out."

"Yeah, sure—fly into the maws of hell, I get it!" Astrid sneered, raking a hand through her disheveled mane. "But aren't you sick of getting your ass kicked, flying solo behind enemy lines in some half-assified hulk that shouldn't even make it back to port? It's time to trust me on this one, boss."

He flinched at the mockery laced in her tone despite himself. Years ago, such petty insubordination might've earned Leo a court martial...and if back then, maybe there really wasn't anything left to salvage. But he knew better now—that edge served only to hide hurt.

"I do trust you," he said finally, and felt a twinge of unease at the unfamiliar huskiness of his own words. "That's why you're my first. But those guns—"

"Are more than just hot pieces of chrome, yeah, I know the drill," Vex interrupted scathingly. "And who cares if we don't walk away from this with skin intact? At least our ship'll live to ram another day!"

Leo's breath hitched involuntarily, shock sparking through his system like a lightning strike. They weren’t thinking the same game. What did she mean by that?

"Oh no," Astrid breathed, green eyes widening with dawning horror as the implications sunk home. "Ohh shit, we can't...it wouldn't...!" Her voice trailed off, face pale, lips parting in dismay.

Astrid's gaze snapped down, pupils dilating as comprehension dawned like sunrise over the scorched horizon of a charred planet. In disbelief, she counted under her breath - three, four, five... The Starlight Marauder was outmatching its pursuers by precisely that margin. A cold thrill ran up her spine, eclipsing the lingering sting of humiliation. This impossible advantage wasn't an accident. She met Leo's intense stare, heart thundering against her ribs.

With exaggerated slowness, Astrid turned to leer at her foe through the viewport. Their armored destroyers looked small and inconsequential against the stark, starspewn backdrop. So vulnerable. A grim smile settled on her lips.

"Looks like someone figured out how to squeeze juice from the ol'laser cannons after all," she purred venomously. "Guess you didn't waste all that fancy spit-and-polish training for nothing, huh soldier boy?" 

Before he could retort, panic flashed across Leo's haunted features. Realization had been writ across them large-as-life - the terrible, thrilling prospect they both faced, yet dreaded grasping. Not just flight, but escape...into utter obscurity, beyond enemy pursuit, where not even the Union Navy's vast resources could follow.

Her breath came in ragged pants while she beheld the same awful beauty reflected in his eyes—freedom, redemptive oblivion riding the solar winds to unknown worlds where their pasts couldn't reach. Yet terror of the yawning, dark abyss loomed large, threatening to swallow hope itself.

"Astrid," Leo implored hoarsely, reaching out to clamp a trembling hand around her elbow. "We can't—we need to be prepared for what happens next!"

"Oh, we'll be ready," she assured him tightly, curling her fingers around his in reponse as he drew her closer. Heat emanated from his touch, igniting a conflagration within her breast that rivaled stars' birth. Her resolve wavered, even as the urge to surrender to fate—and passion—mountained higher with each pounding heartbeat.

"You know exactly what we are now," Astrid whispered, locking gazes with the man beside whom she'd chosen to dare eternity, alone and unmoored amidst celestial expanse. "Just try stopping either of us, huh?"

A fleeting smile touched Leo's lips—a gambler's smirk shared between partners embracing chaos. For an eternal, suspended moment, they balanced poised upon the knife-edge precipice of change, world-weary lovers and outlaw kin, bound together by the threads fates so cynically twisted into an elixir of self-definition.

Then, his free hand whipped up to grab hold of the command yoke just as hers claimed the firing panel’s console. With desperate synchronicity, they wrestled weapons charge sliders into the max-redline settings and slammed their palms onto the thrust initiators together.

The Starlight Marauder bucked and reared like a wild stallion, shields flashing protective scarlets as railgun emplacements charged to point-blank saturation levels. Behind them, the remaining Union Destroyer commanders bellowed panicked orders and cranked their aft banks around, already realizing too late the fatal mistake. Leo fought the stick for every increment of bleed-off velocity until they cleared the destructive umbrella of unleashed broadsides.

The Starlight Marauder shed momentum like a moth leaving flame. Even outnumbered, the pair had purchased precious seconds with that suicidal feint which would stretch to minutes—or hours—if Providence remained kind. Yet time counted for less than nothing in a chase unfolding across hundreds of thousands of kilometers at speeds approaching relativistic thresholds.

Chapter 13 Scene 4 – Armor Pierced

Leo gritted teeth against the raw energy coursing through the command center in pulsating waves, each thrumming vibration echoing through the metal beneath him like divine music reverberating from a great pipe organ. The ship groaned as it heaved forward, protesting the surge of power Vex had keyed into its systems. "Sweet holy gods Above—" he hissed, "if you two're done getting cozy behind the controls, maybe chime in before we get shredded!"

A burst of raucous laughter shot back through the comms link, the bitter edge leavened by a note of triumph. "Now ain't you just the sweetest sounding worrywart!" Vex crowed. "The best entertainment is yet to come, and it doesn't involve us getting splattered like bug squished under heel! Check your long range." 

Obediently, Leo flicked the display over to reveal a mesmerizing sequence of data points streaming across a star chart—detailed telemetry painting a picture more unsettling than any nightmare born of fever dreams. At phenomenal accelerations, the Starlight Marauder was hurtling towards the event horizon of a super-massive black hole, an abyss so dense and heavy that even light quailed in retreat at its impassable boundaries.

"What in...holy Hades?" His knuckles whiten at the sheer audacity of such a maneuver. They were playing a game of cosmic chicken with an astrophysical juggernaut twice the mass of Sol itself. Escape velocities past this point meant defying the very laws of physics. One miscalculation could mean being stretched like taffy between gravitational tidal forces prior to an agonizing quantum disintegration. And still, with every second that ticked by—and he cursed himself for not considering this sooner—the Starlight Marauder gained, shedding distance from its pursuers like vermin fleeing a culling fire.

"We'll skirt the rim," Vex's voice cut through the static humming in his ears, calm and confident despite the sheer lunacy of their flight profile. "That gravity lens will deflect enough of their shots to keep 'em from getting a bead on us while we gap."

"No shit?" Leo snorted incredulity around gritted teeth. "What genius thought this up?"

"A smart one." Her words crackled with proprietary pride as she glanced sidelong at her grinning first mate, Gideon, who sat hunched intently over his own station. Their gazes met briefly in a flash of understanding, locked for only a heartbeat before returning focus to the battle raging beyond the armor-piercing shell of their battered ship.

For an unreasonably long moment, nothing changed—as if frozen on the cusp of catastrophe. Then came the first warning alarm, painting yellow highlights along the outside of the viewport's tinted dome. Leo could almost hear his breath hiss aloud—anticipation bated cruelly short of release by an infinitesimal delay imperceptible save in the throbbing of his racing heart.

Outside, inky blackness parted like a stage curtain descending, revealing the glimmering white silhouette of the black hole stretched into an incandescent pseudoplate against the galaxy's dark canvas. The event horizon appeared closer with each passing nanosecond, swelling outwards and imploding inward simultaneously into a perfect sphere of impossibility. Leo felt the hairs stand along the nape of his neck and the fine membrane of sweat prickle across his scalp in anticipation of impending apotheosis. For they would cross this threshold—that inviolate boundary separating the realms of existence and oblivion.
