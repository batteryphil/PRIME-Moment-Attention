# Chapter 18: Vows in the Dark


Darkness swallowed them whole as Astrid yanked the controls savagely starboard, sending the Starlight Marauder careening past a scorched moon fragment. Leo grunted, crashing back into his seat as the inertial damping struggled to compensate for the rapid slew. Recycled oxygen hissed through the hull – a hollow, metallic sound, punctuated by the rattling stress of strained alloys.

"Brace for—"

But Astrid cut herself short as the warning trembled in her throat unspoken. This close, the fleet gave no grace period before opening fire, their beams stabbing across intervening void with millisecond timing that belied how desperately these hunters sought their prey. A flare of orange-white seared the cockpit windows, spalling shattered fragments onto the command deck with screeching velocity.

"Hold on!" Astrid snarled, hauling the ship through a dizzying series of evasive twists meant to confound guidance systems – not intended to soothe their riders. The G forces flattened Leo against the harness, sweat beading on his brow as he clung to whatever handhold presented itself, barely recognizing the burning latticework of stars through the flickering warnings.

"Leo, check our status," Astrid barked over the cacophony, voice steady despite the chaos. Her hands danced across the command board, patching together damaged conduits under the screaming din of stressed circuits. Somewhere beneath the roar, a crack echoed through the decking, a ominous harbinger of impending failure.

Mercer fought free of his binding webbing, eyes blazing with desperate determination as he pried himself loose from the inertia constraints. Flailing through the turbulent heat of the cockpit, his numb fingers finally found purchase on a console, dragging himself forward to slump beside Astrid at the helm.

"Life Support yellow. Sensors half-functional," Mercer gasped, fighting to breathe against the pressure exerting on his chest plates. Hot rivulets of moisture trickled down his forehead, stinging where cold metal scraped his skin. The acrid tang of superheated plastics and plasma burn seared his nostrils, mixing grotesquely with the sterile musk of emergency oxygen.

Outside, sparks danced wildly as more hits struck home, painting the Starlight Marauder with a hellish corona. Heat shielding glowed an incandescent white before venting in great, explosive whuffs, leaving streaks of black carbonaceous debris trailing from marring plates.

"We're taking too much damage. We can't keep this up," Mercer warned urgently. Astrid bit her lip, considering options rendered treacherous by dwindling resources – or faith in those already spent. Panic threatened to rear its head but she tamped it ruthlessly down, focusing instead on the singular fact anchoring her: Leo Mercer was still by her side.

"It'll have to do," she spat, a hair's breadth from growling those words past clenched teeth. Her gaze fell upon his battered features, face set in lines of grim resolve. Perhaps that was what sealed their fates – their similarities.

Rising gracefully despite the relentless pounding, Astrid moved to stand beside her reluctant co-pilot, reaching out to cup bruised knuckles in her palm. Beneath her touch, Leo's calloused flesh prickled with cool sweat trapped between layers of spacesuit fabric, smearing droplets thinly across her own dry skin in a fleeting moment of tender contact.

"I know what I want," Astrid breathed.

Chapter 18: Vows in the Dark

Beneath scarlet alerts flashing on bulkheads and screens, the Marauder creaked in protest around them – an ominous symphony heralding the starship's mortal fragility. 

"Damn it," Leo snarled over suit comms, craning his neck to peer aft at where the infernal craft once dwelled, now shredded in ruin against vacuum. His eyes narrowed to slits behind glare-resistant optics as he grappled with Astrid's assertion, her voice echoing tinnily within close quarters. How could one man possibly survive such cataclysm?

"We... we didn't kill him," Mercer reiterated stubbornly, jaw flexing with renewed determination under Astrid's unyielding scrutiny.

"Not yet," the pirates retorted wryly, a thread of malice lacing her tone.

"And if they retrieve you—"

"Too late for maybes." Astrid waved off Leo's concerns dismissively with a flick of pale fingers, sending an errant strand of silver-streaked hair dancing against her cheek. She leaned closer, until only a whisper separated their helmets – a futile barrier against the intimate tumult brewing.

"If their tech doesn't vaporize me on the spot, your people will definitely make the trade a necessity," Astrid whispered hoarsely, drawing a rough line through Leo's rigid spine with the tips of her gloves.

He swallowed audibly, face paling perceptibly beneath his reflective visor. Astrid watched the play of conflicting emotions cross his angular features – the stubborn loyalty, the practical acknowledgment, the primal unease. All perfectly natural. In another universe, far removed from the toxic alliance currently binding them...

The pirate woman shifted restlessly, armor creaking soft protests. "A promise ain't worth two huffed creds without a way to back it up, no matter who gives it."

Leo bristled, an eddy of rankled pride surfacing amidst cracks splitting his stoic facade. For a microsecond, she glimpsed the old military commander, ever quick to dispute and right perceived wrongs, even in the most bizarre extremity of events.

Her smirk twitched – yes, precisely the reaction she'd aimed for.

"We're dead men walking, Mercer. At least among our own lot." Her lips, thin and colorless despite the rich red hue of the suit's environment control, curved wickedly above the gold accents framing her irises. Those predatory orbs seemed to glow more luminously in the dim light streaming down from Marauder's ports: an eerie testament to her ferocious vitality. "Don't pretend like there aren't consequences if word of this partnership bleeds into other circles. Reputation precedes loyalty every damned time."

A muscle in Leonardo's cheek pulsed once, twice – then stilled as cool acceptance flooded slowly behind his eyes. The realization settling over him felt curiously similar to the first taste of space's unforgiving gravity, a force so pervasive, it molded a man even in repose until every cell acknowledged its presence. He was done arguing tooth and nail, convinced Astrid would see reason... because she didn't have a choice.

Leo lifted one gloved hand, extended finger by finger. At each point of contact, icy synthetic material adhered with a faint hiss to Astrid's, bonding them at wrists. When finally their palms pressed together, a jolt of static electricity snapped through the link, sparking tiny arcs of blue-white fire.

"You're buying me time."

Leo held fast to Astrid as the Starlight Marauder lurch-shuddered through sudden evasive maneuvers. Alarms screeched, cacophonous white noise assaulting their eardrums via vibro-hearing units since the void itself offered no ulterior soundscape for cover. In the chaos, Mercer found momentary solace within the sheer muscularity of Vex's suited form, locked close at the chest and interlaced arms. The temperature-sensitive fabric clung dampily to exposed skin where their linked hands and forearms rested against the black leather flight suits, exuding the combined tang of antiseptic wipes, the sharpness of Astrid's citrus-spice perfume, and Mercer's own salt-sweat undertone.

"Pyraxi fighters," Astrid ground out, golden-eyed stare narrowing to slits as targeting systems pinged alerts across her HUD. "Four of the bastards closing fast off port quarter. Shields can absorb the initial salvo but—"

"Marauder can't outrun them in short-term," Leo finished grimly, mental calculations already racing through probable scenarios. His index finger twitched against the reinforced webbing of Vex's suit – searching for thrum, feeling for metal-to-metal contact anywhere along their sealed union. "We need to—

"Squirt one, two, three!" Astrid barked orders that sent the Marauder careening sharply starboard while launching targeted missile salvos from concealed weapon arrays. Laser turrets whined up to life, spitting forth lances of charged plasma designed to vaporize armor plating and seek life signatures. The pirate captain's free wrist darted to her comms panel, fingers flying across the holo-keys as she routed sensor feeds to auxiliary stations hidden in the ship's cargo holds and engineering compartments, hoping to keep Pyraxi trackers guessing about Marauder's true configuration.

Asteroid fragments and molten debris from previous dogfights swirled chaotically around the battling vessels, some close enough to clip at empty suitskins or rattle cabin walls but mostly keeping a perilous distance to avoid catastrophic damage. Dust motes danced within the faint illumination leaking through the damaged main shield, suspended in microgravity until attracted by ambient electrostatic charges. Vex's lips skated back in a vicious snarl before she bit them shut, jaw muscles bulging beneath mottled grays and blacks.

"Mercer, I'm starting my turn–wait..."

A lurching shimmy knocked Marauder sideways as something massive impacted her stern bulkhead near the docking bays, sending shockwaves rolling through every piece of the vessel – jarring, bone-juddering, and nauseatingly prolonged. A strangled hiss escaped Vex as if punched in the gut. The hit had ruptured internal airlocks and equalized pressure too quickly throughout decks one through six, making any area prone to decompression.

Astrid slapped the comms unit again, snarling what could have been an oath or encouragement into microphone mesh as she frantically rebalanced maneuvering thrusters, struggling to align sensors with the nearest exit tunnel of Kaldorian-controlled asteroid belt as Pyraxi fighters continued to press their attack unrelentingly. Their relentless pursuit made sense – corner Marauder in open space outside planetary or station defenses, and finishing the raid became merely a matter of timing their final assault. No room for retreat, only elimination.

Adrenaline-sour saliva stung the corners of Vex's mouth, taste buds scorched by the caustic mix of fear, loathing, and desperate concentration coursing through her veins like liquid fire. She strained against the seat restraints, gritting teeth as Pyraxi beam-lances sliced scorch marks across Marauder's port bow while ion thrusters stuttered futilely against pursuit. Sweat trickled down her neck to soak the collar of her flight suit despite zero-G conditions.

"...fighting it," Leo gasped out, his breath hot against the side of Vex's neck. His arms tightened around her waist reflexively when another shockwave ripped through the ship, making his bones rattle. He clapped hands over her ears as if that futile gesture might protect them both from the screaming stress vibrations threatening to crack ship hull alike from within. 

The sudden crunch of metal snapping sent pangs shooting up Leo's forearms searing into his hands at the exact moment he hunched forward, shielding her head against the shattering explosion of a Pyraxi fighter's engines starboard. Vomiting in microgravity left him dizzy and weak as the reek of char and ozone filled his lungs. 

Gasping, retching, he struggled blindly for purchase among torn cables, spilled controls, and splintered console components...and slammed into a wall, a jagged edge scraping a bleeding gash above his brow. Stunned but still clinging obstinately to Vex, his heart pounded in his ears almost loud enough to drown out the creaks of strained frame and groans of stressed metal working to absorb the punishment dealt them. His vision blurred red at the edges as a rivulet of blood ran down past his eye, leaving a salty, warm trail.

He wanted - needed - to look up at the sky, to see how much farther they would be dragged under the gunsights of enemies before Kaldorians finally delivered them a lifeline. But couldn't tear gaze away from her profile as it ghosted past, pale blue eyes haunted as ghostly shadows reflected the crimson emergency lighting. In this frozen slice of time suspended between battle and death, the war between mercenary and soldier faded into insignificance next to the raw fear of the abyss yawning darkly ahead.

The ship gave a long lamentable groan as a section of deckplates lifted underfoot like ice shelves in meltdown - Marauder's main propulsion systems dying agonizing deaths, spilling their guts in glowing chunks of ablative insulation and superheated hydrazine fuel amidst the carnage. Alarms screamed for attention, flashing lights strobing in pulsing rhythms that drove through pain to reach that most primal of senses - hearing.

"Leo?" Vex breathed against his lips, voice as hollow as a child's toy telephone after static clearance. For an age, there was only the hissing of life support air recycling, the wet chug of external thermal radiators shedding heat, and the eternal sigh of space cold embracing their damaged craft like a lover. Then, faint, a distant rumble. Thunder muted by distance and the oppressive vacuum.

Astrid slowly released the comms microphone, letting it droop heavy toward the buckled floor plates. She turned her face into the crook of Leo's neck, lips brushing his skin as if seeking some shred of vital warmth in their frozen hell amidst the shattered deck ruins and burning shreds of Pyraxi debris trailing behind the crippled Starlight Marauder.
