# Chapter 4: Radiation Lockout

RADIATION LOCKOUT

Adrenaline erupted through Leo's veins like a meteor strike as sensors screamed alarm. "Shields failing!" he bellowed over straining speakers as emergency klaxons wailed their shrill cadence.

Beside him, Vex flung a furious glance at her console. Cold dread seeped into her gut as she realized why. A blinding flash lit the cramped command room—the shields disengaging to unleash a torrent of energy onto unsuspecting armor plating. The reaction was instantaneous and catastrophic.

"FIRE SHUTTLES NOW!"

Leo hauled himself away from his station, muscles screaming protest even as the shockwave slammed into the Marauder like a physical blow. The ship creaked and groaned, struts singing a chorus of protest. Alarms shrieked louder still as the superstructure swayed precariously under the onslaught.

The hatch irised open, revealing the lead-lined tunnel leading forward to the storm shelter. Vex grabbed Leo's arm, hauling him through the narrow opening just as metal screamed beneath external pressures. The blast doors sealed shut behind them, their heavy mass slamming home with seismic force.

Exhausted, battered, bodies pressed uncomfortably close in the confined space of the decontamination room, Vex risked a glance at her companion. Damp, tangled hair framed Leo's ashen face. Blood oozed sluggishly from a cut above one eyebrow, dripping cold rivulets down the planes of his cheekbones, mingling with streaks of oil and grime.

"What was that?" he asked hoarsely, his breath hot against her ear. They were standing shoulder-to-shoulder within the tight confines, body heat creating localized pockets of warmer air amidst the bitter chill of the containment space.

"Somebody getting my attention," Vex bit out between clenched teeth. She scrubbed bloody fingers across a scratched cheekbone, smearing crimson across pallid skin. "Guess I'll have company soon enough when you lot figure out how to get your shit together."

Leo's lips twisted wryly underneath a dark beard etching lines of fatigue around his eyes. "Charming, as always." His voice sounded muffled in her proximity with the helmets sealing shut around their heads. "Can't wait to see who gets sent to babysit the relic ex-pilot from ancient Earth and his...compelling personality."

For a moment, something flickered behind those piercing blue eyes, swift and secret as meteors across zenith skies—but vanished before she could identify what precisely sparked it. Vex simply snorted, annoyed at herself for even glancing so longingly at that unattainable sparkle. This wasn't about romantic entanglements or fleeting connections; it was survival.

With slow deliberateness, Vex retracted the helmet seal until mere micro-inches separated mouth from a familiar ear. Soft, rasping speech washed over his skin once more.

"Just keep talking," she husked quietly, "let me know anyone else is coming. Until then, I'm gonna enjoy this reprieve—away from radiation suits and malfunctioning hull plating."

Underneath the bulkheads, a muffled pounding echoed through the ship, marking the relentless persistence of those outside threats trying to claw their way past Marauder defenses. The very thought made Vex smirk inwardly. Good luck to them; star captains don't become legends by yielding.

Within the cocoon of shared closeness, both warriors retreated into stillborn fantasies woven from desperation and exhaustion—the stark lines and angles of metal giving succor to imaginations overwhelmed by isolation.

Radiation Lockout

Silence fell swiftly in its wake, punctuated only by the occasional crackle-spark of systems cycling offline in response to the breaches. Through hazy cabin air thick with acrid smoke, a sudden scent wave hit: ozone mingling with blood, burnt metal, and the pungent tang of Mercer's own singed uniforms fabric. Astrid blinked away the dazzle left by her proximity flare, peering down at her battered confederate to gauge severity of the injury. His eyes were slitted against the stinging brightness filtering in through fractured armorplast panels, face pinched white beneath the dark stubble.

"Bad luck getting clipped early there, Commander – didn't mean to open a welcoming committee for the outer dark just yet," Astrid murmured wryly, her tone carefully pitched for reassurance rather than mockery. She kept her voice low, not wanting to waste precious atmosphere on unnecessary volume. "Guess it's just you and me dancing with the alien scum again...at least we won that last exchange," she added pointedly, gesturing toward smoldering crater marks seared into the nearest bulkhead where a pursuing hull had kissed flak once too often.

Mercer grunted, coughing weakly against the smoke haze. A bead of crimson welled up between his gritted teeth. "Pirates, pirates, pirates..." he growled half-heartedly, forcing out a hoarse chuckle that ended with a pained gasp. Astrid held her breath, watching anxiously as she gently lifted his chin to peer into the wound between his lips. Despite the jagged tear, it looked shallow enough—and already clotting. Still, the pallor of that thin complexion set off alarm bells in her mind.

"Hold still, let me get this bandaged." Gently but swiftly, Astrid stripped torn scraps of uniform fabric from the first aid kit, working efficiently under minimal lighting. Her touch was surprisingly tender despite their antagonistic history, betraying an unconscious empathy masked by calloused exterior. In turn, Mercer accepted each careful pressure and tug with grateful resignation, body sagging against her supporting weight while he fought to maintain vision without passing out. Their nearness created an intimacy born of necessity, two damaged predators pressed flush against the throbbing heart of war.

Astrid secured the makeshift compress and tied it off with practiced knots, taking a step back to survey the damage done to Mercer and her stolen ship alike. Her gaze raked mercilessly over buckled floor panels, split seams leaking vapor, the telltale wisps suggesting oxygen leaks that needed patching immediately before they evolved into uncontainable infernos.

"Well, looks like we've got our work cut out for us, mercifully minus alien parasites for the moment," Astrid commented dryly, jerking a thumb over her shoulder towards the tangled wreckage of what was once part of her proud starship hanging askew in the breach. A faint groan escaped the straining superstructure as metal tired and buckled further under external stress.

Before Mercer could respond, warning klaxons began blaring throughout the chamber once more, painting everything in gaudy red light. Astrid cursed under her breath as the deckplate heaved beneath their feet again, shaking loose the acrid dust of pulverized plastics left behind by yesterday's conflicts. Overlaid atop the alarm frequency pulsed a different signal—the unmistakable thrum of boarding parties breaching the next layer of defense.

"All hell's breaking loose out there," Astrid spat grimly, tucking an errant lock of hair behind her ear and cracking her knuckles in anticipation, a gesture.

Astrid surged forward with explosive speed, heedless of the buckling deckplating and yawning breaches along the way. One hand snagged a nearby toolbox while the other ripped down emergency patches and sealing film from alcoves. She pitched them fluidly at Mercer as she charged onward. 

"The suits are breached, Leo! Seal up those gaps yourself, gotta get these outer layers closed off—stats say we've got minutes max till the pressure hull fails!" Astrid bellowed over the shrieking alarm tones.

Her words sent terror coursing through Mercer's veins as she obeyed reflexively, hands moving with panicked expertise over scorched sealants and frayed connections. Despite her best efforts, it was clear many breaches were too vast, too numerous to seal alone using these flimsy patches. The Starlight Marauder shuddered again, throwing them off balance as new sections gave way.

Mercer spotted a telltale tremor resonating across the hull—the reverberations of an attack shuttle piggy-backing onto the crippled cruiser. Panic reanimated Astrid's gaze as she realized they hadn't been its only quarry.

"Avoid-avoid!"

She lunged bodily against Mercer, knocking her sprawling downwind as a searing blast rocked the corridor behind them. A section of bulkhead exploded outward, engulfing Astrid in a whirling storm of smoke, sparks, and splintered metal plating. She screamed, muffled by the cacophony of disintegrating debris as her body shielded Mercer from the lethal shockwave.

With agonizing slowness, Astrid's battered form drifted backwards, trailing smoke, blood, and what seemed like impossible amounts of shredded synthetic fabric from where chunks had torn away. Eyes rolling wildly in their sockets, she fixed Leo with a bloodied snarl even as shattered teeth clicked together ineffectually inside her slack mouth.

"Don't you EVER...leave me...behind."

Her voice faded to a whisper as consciousness abandoned her, sinking beneath the surface leaving nothing but brittle silence and the acrid stench of burned wiring.

Mute, Mercer stared at Astrid's inert form in horror-stricken disbelief. As if operating autonomously, his own hands reached for her, brushing smoke-tainted curls from her bruised face with trembling gentleness. Tears blurred his vision, hot trails tracking down cheeks chapped by the cold vacuum outside. He pressed his forehead to hers, sharing breaths until hers became his.

Time hemorrhaged senselessly as the injured, nearly lifeless woman clung to him like a lost child seeking comfort after a terrible nightmare. Only the constant drumming of footsteps growing louder signaled approaching rescue—and more immediate threats not yet neutralized.

Heart pounding, Mercer broke contact last—pulling away to scan their surroundings for danger. Smoke still billowed from the gaping hole where Astrid had taken cover, obscuring any farther attackers. Sirens wailed incessantly overhead while the air tasted heavy with chemicals meant to disperse noxious gases. With renewed focus, he assessed their condition—a long list of injuries with none acute enough to kill them right then.

They were alive—not even seriously so. Yet in this instant, sheltered together amidst chaos and carnage, something profound shifted irreversibly between commander and corsair—forged in fury and desperation within the brutal crucible of war.

Their lips met in a fierce, messy kiss, passion and relief commingling as their entwined figures struggled to rise amidst scattered debris. For one perfect moment, Mercer forgot every hurt past or threat future—all that existed was Vex's warmth, her gasping laughter, her living, breathing presence enfolding.

✦ ✦ ✦

Commander Mercer stood swaying slightly, arms cinching tightly around Astrid to keep her upright amid the smoke-choked wreck of the engineering access tunnel. Blasting the internal atmosphere to bits hadn't eliminated the acrid haze, which stung their raw throats and irritated eyes. As they staggered forward, boots crunching over shattered plastic and metallic shards, sirens shrieked in protest from the ship's emergency systems. Alarms flickered crimson across console displays in corridors they passed—the Starlight Marauder groaning under the assault of war.

Mercer guided Astrid along, a pillar of strength and reassurance forged from sheer adrenaline and unshakable determination. Each labored step took on an almost ritualistic quality—celebrating the fact neither of them bleeds out here, this very minute, nor succumbs to cardiac arrest from trauma-induced stress. Together they wove through the maze of catwalks and grated bulkheads, dodging panicked Marines who rushed to seal breaches and repress life support failures triggered by the explosion.

As they neared Sickbay, Astrid finally managed a cracked, rasping laugh against Mercy's shoulder—broken but unmistakable. "Guess you made a good spotter after all." Her voice roughened by smoke inhalation sounded almost endearing to him in that moment. A surprising contrast to her usual brusqueness.

"Try to stay upright," he advised gruffly in return, planting firm hands on her hips to steady her against the wall before they crossed the entrance of Med Bay. Already a swarm of medical staff in silver suits converged upon them, shouting instructions and deploying diagnostic scanners with practiced efficiency. The medics worked to stabilize Astrid first, attaching oxygen masks and IV drips while examining a dizzying multitude of abrasions, contusions, and lacerations hidden beneath her blood-soaked robes.

Mercer watched anxiously, heart still racing, as she was wheeled away on a hover gurney towards treatment bays. More than just a fleeting worry tugged at his chest at leaving her side already—but other dire risks to the ship demanded his attention too. He gave the medic closest to hand a curt nod and spun about, striding out into the main passageways again.

Aft section reports were grim—lifeboats prepped but damage to external docking arrays hampered egress. Engineering claimed minimal propulsion capability left, but the shields held surprisingly strong considering how little armor remained intact. Intel revealed incoming fleet formations closing fast on Marauder's damaged flanks. Even as they reconstituted forces and tended the numerous wounded, they knew those reinforcements would be upon them within a scant thirty minutes.

Mercer swallowed hard, mind reeling over the choices remaining. Fight harder in hopes of repelling the imminent onslaught? Attempt escape into hyperspace and risk crashing someone behind at the fringes of the jump? Or buy a few precious hours negotiating with Fleet HQ in open channels before those guns thundered around them?

Just deciding what course to chart proved agonizingly complex—no simple solution granted this tightrope balancing act between desperate survival and desperate hope...as reality hung suspended between utter disaster and a faint chance at salvation.

And through it all, only one certainty crystallized in Leo Mercer's soul, imprinted there across every hammer-hewn ridge of anguish:

He'd walk into hell beside Astrid 'Vex' Ross, through any firestorm of plasma, if it meant sparing even an atom of her untamed life force—his protector, partner, and...something more, born brutally honest beneath the cold vacuum of battle.
