# Chapter 17: Casualties of the Void

They were a single bad impulse away from oblivion, hurtling towards the waiting behemoth at point-blank range - two insignificant flecks of stardust against the massive hull looming large in the viewscreen. Heat shields already strained redline, overloaded thrusters groaning under impossible stresses as Captain Astrid Ross wrestled the crippled Marauder onto a collision course.

Leo gripped the edge of his acceleration couch, jaw clenched white as he braced against the violent pitch and yaw. This couldn't possibly end well. Not even close. "Astrid, what the fuck?!" he yelled over the cacophony of wailing alarms and tortured metal. "We're going to splat ourselves against that thing!"

The pirate captain's reply was grim, her focus laser-sharp on lining up the strike. "No choice anymore. We either get swallowed whole, spread across light-years to decorate some long-dead galactic sphincter - or make this bastard our own personal hole."

"You've gone insane," Leo spat, voice cracking despite himself as star-stuff turned to furnace heat painting the bow viewports a molten crimson. 

The impact came as one, a tremendous shockwave rippling back though hull plates and skeletal supports, throwing crew into wild tangos - bruised backs slamming against consoles, loose cargo shifting madly in zero-g chaos.

Through the storm of disorientation, Astrid clung tenaciously to her command seat, riding out brutal oscillations triggered by glancing hits. Main drives howled their death agonies then faded abruptly as primary fuel injectors ruptured outward in a shower of sparks. Emergency power kept them from total darkness but barely. Dim red lights cast eerie shadows as decks groaned and shifted ominously beneath their feet.

Leo struggled to climb free amidst the wreckage scattered across the bridge, hauling himself over broken equipment until he stumbled to his knees by Astrid's side. She'd slumped forward, arms draped limply over the tactical station, hair plastered to her skull in sweat and grime.

"Vex," Leo breathed, gently pulling her upright to meet his concerned gaze. Her eyes were unfocused, staring into something only she could see.

"Gotta...move," she mumbled, shaking off his touch with sudden ferocity. "Before-"

A dull rumble thrummed through the hull plates, low but intensifying. Astrid cut off with a wince, squinting wearily at flickering sensor data. Even as crew reports trickled in, most comms knocked out or jammed, the gravity of their situation became gut-wrenchingly clear: they had indeed smashed themselves halfway into the derelict's rusting, pitted flank. And that ruddy beast of old ships didn't seem pleased.

"We're pinned," Astrid said bluntly, voice numb with defeat and dread. "No getting out unless that hull gives way...and I'd rather eat my boots than count on the whims of some ancient wreck." She took a slow breath, visibly fighting down the urge to start screaming. "Cargo bay's probably the only place to try and patch ourselves up. If we play our cards right, might just live long enough for the ESO to find us..."

Leo looked at Astrid in disbelief. They were stranded in a hostile metal tomb, bleeding air and coolant by the minute. "Yeah...because nothing says peaceful rescue like a panicked evacuation from a hulk teetering on planetary-scale destruction."

"Just shut up and follow me," Astrid snapped, already unclipping from her chair and stumbling toward the descending lift tunnel.

Astrid staggered through the darkening corridors, her boots scraping against torn deckplates. Every step sent jarring pain lancing up her leg where shards of ceramic armor had bitten deep into flesh and muscle. It felt like walking through a frozen nightmare.

Mercer limped alongside her, jaw clenched tight as he clutched his side. Blood seeped slowly between his gloved fingers, leaving crimson smears on his environment suit. With each labored exhale, he winced, face paler than before under the emergency lighting.

The eerie silence within the battered Starlight Marauder was broken only by the soft whirrs of damaged systems and their ragged breathing. Panic threatened to boil over, but Astrid kept it leashed, forcing herself to focus. 

They needed to reach the cargo bay without delay – the ship's heart still pumped life-support air into the main sections, at least for now. But every second bought them precious room to maneuver if the hull cracked open or worse.

"You okay?" Astrid asked gruffly, not expecting a sincere reply. Her hand shot out to steady Leo as he swayed on his feet.

"Just peachy," he gurgled bitterly, eyes squeezing shut against the pain. "Getting reacquainted with my ex-wife, she's been trying to kill me for weeks now..."

Astrid snorted. A thin rim of ice encased her bruised heart, but beneath there remained a spark – maybe fondness, maybe something more. Could never tell anymore. All that mattered was the next few vital minutes as they fought for their lives against the relentless void.

Together they stumbled onward until reaching the cargo bay. A haze of cooling vapor hung heavy, mingling with the acrid reek of scorched wiring. Three grav tethers dangled inert near an overturned crate, discarded by hurried escapees probably.

"Grab those," Astrid ordered curtly, dragging herself toward the battered medbay setup near the bulkhead. Medkits, bandages, and antiseptic wipes were scattered about, mostly undamaged – yet another testament to a harrowed crew struggling to keep their ghost ship operational.

Within the cramped confines of the tiny infirmary, Astrid began applying pressure dressings and running diagnostics on the wounded duo. Merciless medical AI chirped at her to sedate Mercer, claiming he likely suffered internal bleeding, but she refused – wouldn't risk the pain numbing his reflexes when they needed him alert.  

Leo grimaced through gritted teeth watching Astrid work, wondering how someone so tough could hide such deft hands under her calloused exterior. He forced himself to focus instead on their dismal situation.

"So what's the plan, Captain Pirate?" he croaked around a sudden spasm of agony in his abdomen. "Let's hear your illustrious strategy for getting out alive..."

Astrid froze mid-bandage, eyes narrowing. "Oh, it's simple really. We've got maybe an hour tops until this scrapheap implodes or the leak drains our air completely – either way, game over. So our choices are limited to finding something to weld a breach with, ripping apart furniture to reinforce weak points, or—"

"Pretend to send out a distress signal to lure in rescuers while playing dead in stasis pods?"

"Yeah, well, that wasn't in my original plans. Let's just say the circumstances have become rather...unprecedented." Astrid's lips twisted wryly. "How's your stomach holding up? You look like you could vomit up all the lead weights strapped to your ankles."

Leo waved away concern, glaring.

✦ ✦ ✦

Leo's gaze lingered on Astrid, captivated by the ruthless gleam in her eyes, the firm set of her mouth as she detailed their dire situation. His stomach churned queasily, acid burning his throat, but he forced down bile, swallowing the bitter taste of defeat alongside recriminations for letting things spiral so perilously out of control.

"I'm fine," he growled, hating how brittle his voice sounded, the underlying trembling he couldn't quite mask. "Not exactly feeling festive, but we've got bigger problems than my gut right now." Astrid flicked her silver-flecked bangs back, meeting his fierce glare with one of her own. "I'd argue that's an understatement," she snapped, fingers drumming a rapid tattoo against her thigh. 

Leo followed her agitated motion with his eyes, drawn inexorably to touch that sinewy muscle, imagine trailing kisses along its corded length. He jerked his gaze upward, face heating, only to collide with Astrid's piercing violet stare, the intensity nearly bowling him over again. What was happening to him?

A knock at the bulkhead disrupted the charged atmosphere, jolting them both back to reality. "Ship systems are experiencing catastrophic failures across the board!" The panicked cry of Helm Officer Dax came muffled through reinforced metal, underscored by ominous groans and screeches. 

"Heat exchangers offline, cooling coils ruptured––" Astrid cut him off, already moving towards the door. "Mercer, keep working on that patch – you're closer to the hull breach. I'll assess the damage control stations and see if any salvageable parts can be rerouted." Her tone brooked no argument as she ripped open the seal, plunging into the fray beyond.

Leo bit back a snarl, not liking being left behind while Astrid charged headlong into chaos, but knew better than to second-guess her in crisis mode. With a sigh rough with despair, he hauled himself upright, limbs heavy and uncoordinated, and turned his attention back to the stubborn seam girdling airlock three. Sweat-soaked hair clung to his skull, his shirt soaked through with perspiration, clinging revealingly to his lean torso—but none of that mattered compared to the countdown ticking away in his mind...

*50 minutes remaining*

Grasping wrench after wrench, Leo wielded brute force, channeling adrenaline into every blow as he battled the obstinate chunk of warped aluminum. If he didn't crack it open soon, they were both dead men walking. Suddenly, hot pain seared his thumb, and merciful darkness blossomed at the edges of his vision. Clutching the wounded digit, Leo struggled to maintain a grip with his other hand as spots danced before his eyes...

Just then, Astrid burst back in, visor retracted, face etched with frustration. She paused upon spotting Leo, hovering precipitously near blackout, and hurried to him with a curse. "For fuck's sake, Mercer! Are you intentionally pushing yourself too far?" In the distance, the hull shuddered ominously beneath starlit blackness.

"I think he passed out a few seconds ago," Astrid shouted to Dax over her shoulder, catching Leo halfway as he started to slump. Strapping a flexible first-aid pad around his throbbing thumb, she hoisted him effortlessly, muscles rippling beneath her flight suit as she swung him upside-down by his feet. 

"You might be right about heading to medbay anyway," Dax panted in response, frantically fiddling with readouts.

Chapter 17 – Casualties of the Void, Scene 4

Astrid whirled Leo’s dangling form around, her breath misting the viewport as an unobstructed vista of stardust sprawled beyond the ship's ragged puncture wound. Against the inky velvet expanse, brilliant spirals of aurora borealis laced through the void - ionization trails etched by nearby supernovae or perhaps the Marauder's own superheated plasma from the recent close call. A shiver ran down Astrid's spine, even as concern for her unlikely companion took precedence.

“Wake up, Leo,” she growled close to his maskless face, shaking him lightly. His unfocused gaze drifted slowly upward, bleary violet eyes gradually focusing on hers. Astrid kept her expression stern, not wanting sympathy to undermine his already precarious mental state if he sensed any softness.

After a tense moment, Leo blinked several times and swallowed dryly. “Did I... pass out again?” he rasped, voice hoarse from breathing thin atmos. He glanced past Astrid at the glittering celestial view and sucked in a sharp breath, struggling to stand upright.

The faint hum of a proximity alert caught Astrid off guard, her attention suddenly split between Leo relearning gravity at an alarming rate and rapidly approaching unknown contacts. She snagged his arm just as he stumbled, steadying him as she scanned the tactical display. The ship's perimeter was flaring red - masses of smaller vessels swarming, cutting off escape routes with menacing precision.

Leo’s foggy eyes snapped into focus as he struggled for balance against her firm grip. “Is that… a fleet? Did we trigger some kind of trap?”

Astrid squeezed out a tight-lipped smile, pride mingling with her fear for them both. "Not entirely your fault this time." She spared a look over her shoulder, nodding curtly at Dax's frantic efforts to analyze their predicament. With no clear shields left intact, the pirated data terminals had become an instant death sentence once hacked. "Let's hope our clever tricks hold up against pursuit." Turning fully back to Leo, she lifted an eyebrow critically as he wobbled but resisted falling. "Looks like you get another taste of the Marauder's acceleration – we need to shift course, fast. Medbay will have to wait if our pursuers catch us."

At her warning, Leo stiffened, teeth gritted. Something flickered behind his eyes – determination rather than defeat. He met Astrid's calculating stare, and together their gazes flicked to the swirling vortex outside. Within its shimmering heart resided a tantalizing secret only a madwoman would chase...

And he just might give in to the temptation.
