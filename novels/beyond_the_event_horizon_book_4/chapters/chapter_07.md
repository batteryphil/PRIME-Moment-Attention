# Chapter 7: The Pilot in the Shadows

Sovereigns of the Dark (Chapter 7: Scene 1)

As the Marauder carved a wild gyre through the storm-fractured void, Commander Leo Mercer crouched amid wreckage in a cramped, claustrophobic alleyway of a maintenance shaft. Pools of oil shimmered around his boots, reflecting a sickle slice of bloodred starlight seeping past tattered insulation panels. He clutched a makeshift mallet in one hand and wrenched himself deeper into the shadows using grip pads as footholds on the slick metal decking. Each pull brought him closer to where he hoped to find sanctuary - a sealed hatch to a larger, pressurized storage compartment.

Inching forward was agonizingly slow work given the centrifugal forces still yanking him side to way due to the battered cruiser's erratic flight paths. Sweat trickled down his temple in glistening rivulets as his exertions rapidly soaked through the thin flight suit beneath grubby coveralls. But Leo dared not pause or relinquish his perch on the maintenance rungs for any protracted duration lest his compromised shielding become visible specks against that lurid smear of aurora outside.

Leo's skin crawled at the awful realization he was completely reliant on the acrobatics of this ancient hulk alone for protection. Any lapse in vigilance could spell doom, since his external armor plating lacked the power and directional capabilities needed to reliably deflect incoming energy or even kinetic strikes. The thought made him queasy. Their options grew more perilous by the minute as desperate evasive maneuvers pushed the ship to its operational limits. Even if she could've matched speeds to dodge incoming attacks outright, which seemed impossible given their dwindling propellant reserves...

A nearby clang from further along the access tunnel snapped Leo back to stark awareness. Some unknown intruder, likely another member of Vex's crew, was paralleling his advance. The pirate's brutal efficiency would ensure they were hot on his heels and about to burst into his hiding spot.

He had mere seconds left.

Scanning frantically, Leo spotted a recessed console tucked away from casual view midway through the alcove ahead, its surface etched with buttons, switches, and data feeds. Heart hammering, he skittered across exposed pipes, clambered up a support strut, and latched onto the narrow ledge beside it. Gripping the cool metal, Leo kicked aside a few stray tools stored on the ledge to conceal his presence.

As expected, the first pirate rounded the bend, twin plasma carbines aimed at knee level and ready to unleash a hailstorm of razor-edged particles upon sighting the intruder. Her gaze swept over the passage blank-faced until Leo froze, praying to any god listening that his silhouette blended with the dark recesses.

She paused several long beats before setting off again, moving deliberately yet warily down the catwalk toward where Leo lurked less than three meters behind. His lungs burned with the need to expel the pent-up air. Every molecule stilled in expectation.

Time unfolded agonizingly like a cheap holovid stuck on repeat. 

Then suddenly, an explosive series of clangs shook the very walls as the Marauder's attackers slammed into the cruiser simultaneously from multiple angles. A tremendous shockwave reverberated down the maintenance passage, slamming Leo against the bulkheads and forcing out a grunt between clenched teeth from the impact.

The pirate woman careened wildly into view a split-second later, jerking hard against the violent tug of inertial dampeners struggling to compensate for the assault. Her aim wavered and swung erratically off-line as she fought to regain balance amidst the jarring turbulence.

The blast slammed her body hard against the durasteel rail, wrenching it from her grip. Astrid flailed wildly, struggling to correct her trajectory before a particularly savage shockwave threw her like a rag doll. A series of percussive thumps echoed through the passageway as projectiles tore into the hull around her, scorching her suit with flying debris whenever her wild movements brought her close enough.

Leo seized the opportunity, launching himself forward in a fluid motion. His fingers closed almost imperceptibly around the carbine still clutched in Vex's hand, muscles coiling as he prepared to disarm her before the next attack wave hit.

She sensed him mere heartbeats away and jerked sideways, avoiding capture by scant centimeters. In one smooth stroke, she whipped the weapon up and around, driving the muzzle savagely into the flesh beneath his chin. Merciless pressure forced his head back while his own momentum carried him stumbling into her.

"Looking for trouble, spaceman?" Astrid snarled through gritted teeth, her breath hot against his ear. "Seems you're barking up the wrong ship." The steel-spring tenseness in her stance warned that a single misstep would end his life in a crimson splash across the corridor.

Every fiber of Leo's being urged surrender – reason screamed in vain as primitive instinct took hold. He met the merciless blue blaze of her gaze, jaw clenching around some guttural retort. Pride stiffened his spine even in defeat at her hands. His face tightened like stretched leather in a grimace that might have been challenge or pain. Perhaps both.

"You're...making this harder," he rasped, words slurred through the molar-crushing pressure. The gun muzzle trembled ever so slightly along the vulnerable underside of his jaw. "For us...both." 

Astrid's arm began to quiver; her knuckles whitened further around the hilt. She stared into his eyes, searching, questioning whether there was anything left of the man beyond defiant bravery and stubborn will. Any hint of the pilot who had somehow survived being marooned centuries ahead of his time. Anyone home behind those feral gray irises pinning her in a furious struggle of wills?

A fleeting flicker—no more substantial than the ghost of a memory—or did Leo's glance slip to the ancient tattoo encircling her wrist? That enigmatic symbol that spoke to secrets far older than either of their kind. Secrets hidden in the unbreathable depths of a dying starlight sea...

In that ephemeral moment, something shifted within Vex. An involuntary softening crept into her expression—the faint whisper of a recollection maybe, or perhaps a strange kinship echoing across unfathomable chasms of space and time. It lasted an instant longer than a stolen heartbeat before she snapped back rigid and remote.

No, such thoughts were weak indulgence, a luxury they could ill afford in the heat of battle. Astrid relaxed marginally yet retained her vise-like grip, poised to slam down if needed. In fact, perhaps a firmer rein was warranted now. There would be no forgiving blunders made under pressure. Only survival counted.

"Tighten up your focus, astronaut," Vex growled, tone dripping disdain. "This isn't a training exercise." Her voice crackled like static in the midst of ongoing chaos. The rumble of attacks grew closer, the air thickening with acrid smoke as burning wreckage clogged the corridors.

Her free hand drifted casually to the small blaster riding her thigh, palm itching for its solid bulk and raw destructive potential.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Starlight Marauder bucked and lurchedList from hit after punishing impact as the ragged squadron of corporate killers pinned them in place. Wails of breached hull plates and shrieks of tortured superstructure screamed above the din of cannon fire. Through it all, Mercer gripped his console with white-knuckled intensity that bordered on fanaticism.

"We can't take much more of this!" he barked at Astrid. "They're targeting our engines, trying to cripple us!"

Her gaze remained fixed out on the swirling vortex below, calculating trajectories with precision born of countless simulations—a pirate's instincts honed by years of navigating deadly asteroid fields untouchably distant in relative terms to Earthlings like him. And yet, despite the vast gulf between their backgrounds, she felt instinctively he understood—knew what the loss of their forward momentum meant.

Their pursuit of the fleeing Manticore was already tenuous under optimal conditions. Stripped of delta-v reserves, any hope of catching worldliner-class vessels receded to absurd impossibilities beyond even Astrid's exceptional piloting prowess. Without the ability to maneuver or dodge, the Marauder became nothing more than floating meat for her relentless predators to tear apart at leisure.

In that existential crucible, a peculiar stillness settled over Vex. Like a warrior in meditation preparing to face a death dance, she stilled every distracting thought until only the crisp taste of frigid vacuum, the hum of systems vital to mere survival, and the harsh whisper of her breathing filled her senses. 

Mercer felt the abrupt shift in her composure almost palpably. Gone was that hint of fleeting vulnerability; it had been supplanted by a crystalline calm, cold and impenetrable as glacier ice. He didn't doubt she'd kill him without hesitation if faced with a terminal threat. Yet an unsettling part of him wondered if there might someday exist circumstances where she allowed him even a precarious toehold in that icy fortress around her heart.

Before such ruminations could further unravel him, his copilot slammed her hand onto his shoulder, grounding him back amidst the chaos. For better or worse, the stakes were uncompromisingly high—and there was scant room for distraction.

"Get focused, spaceman," she snapped, voice razor-edged and cutting through the turbulent din. "We're not in some lab simulator anymore, and I don't have patience for mistakes!"

As if punctuating her reprimand, the ship lurched wildly once more beneath them, throwing both commandos against their restraints. Alarms blared in shrill crescendo while sparks showered and emergency lights strobed ominously.

Astrid's gaze whipped toward the damage control display, her expression coalescing into lethal contempt mingled with undisguised anger. "Those arrogant bastards..." she snarled venomously. "Playing a deadly game with lives they clearly value less than components."

With deft fingers, she spun the dial on h er mounted blaster open, thumbing off its safety as an instinctive reaction. The familiar heft of the weapon settled reassuringly in her grasp—an extension of her armoured will that always held the promise of devastating retaliation whenever push came to shove.

That tangible manifestation of raw power seeped into her skin, feeding Astrid's battle-lust while also sharpening her senses into finely-honed strike weapons themselves. In her eyes, cold calculation now warred with smoldering fury—each facet intertwined in a deadly symphony destined to reshape this lethal scenario to suit her merciless design...

✦ ✦ ✦

Leo was jolted viciously sideways, the force of it slamming him back into his harness hard enough to drive the breath from his lungs in a violent gasp. He struggled to right himself despite the relentless turbulence lashing at the damaged Starlight Marauder like a maelstrom. The cacophonous din blasting through the command center reached a deafening crescendo, obliterating coherent thought. Sirens wailed in overlapping shrieks while a blizzard of sparks rained down in dazzling bursts from torn wiring, bathing them in stark white flashes.

He risked a glance at Astrid, her angular features locked into a mask of pure, murderous rage, eyes blazing with an icy fire fueled by the promise of vengeance. Her gloved hand rested atop the stock of her mounted blaster, the weapon openly armed as a symbol of raw power and capacity for violence aimed squarely at any who dared challenge them.

Through the chaos, Leo caught glimpses of flickering damage control schematics plastered across her console, each new update revealing fresh horrors to their ship's battered state. Debris field collisions, breached compartments, critical systems failing—the picture painted was grim, to say the least.

Astrid's attention remained laser-focused on those damning blue lines mapping out areas under pressure, tracing severed power conduits, and highlighting structural vulnerabilities. Every wrinkle of concentration etched her features told of a mind whirring furiously with tacit strategies and brutal solutions. Her lip curled in wordless disgust at the thoughtless carnage left in their wake.

"Bastards..." the corsair spat low, a tone rougher than sandpaper and coated with the bitter tang of seething contempt. The air seemed to thicken around her, weighted with barely contained aggression simmering just below the surface.

Without breaking eye contact from the monitors, Astrid slotted an ammunition pod into her sidearm with practiced efficiency before seating it firmly. The sharp clack echoed starkly across the battle-worn deckplates, each resonant note underscoring the escalating tempo of confrontation to come.

In her hands, the revolver became an extension of Astrid's deadly intent, every curve and balance point a calculated harmony poised to unleash unfathomable devastation should circumstances necessitate. Its weight solidified in her grip as if anointed by her unyielding will—part equalizer, part instrument of ruthless retribution.

That potent aura radiating from both weapon and wielder rippled outward to enfold everything within reach, subtly altering the very fabric of the command center to reflect a space where brutality reigned supreme and mercy had no place. Even the air felt charged with electricity tinged by the heady mix of fear and bloodlust surging through them both.

Time hung suspended in the heart of the tempest, seconds elongating into an eternity teetering on the precipice between survival and utter annihilation. Both commander and marauder hovered there, balanced precariously between controlled fury and impulsive destruction, poised eagerly to rend their universe apart on the whims of star-faring warfare. Nothing in existence could stand against the explosive fury that brewed between man and woman, technology and chaos—it was simply a matter of which of them would prove the most formidable as the cosmos conspired against the very breath in their chests once more.
