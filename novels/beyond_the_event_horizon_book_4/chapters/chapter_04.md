# Chapter 4: The Ambush at Gate Three

Captain Astrid 'Vex' Ross clung to her command chair, violet-gray hair whipping wild as the Marauder shuddered from bow to stern. Her cybernetic implants flared with warnings, a stark counterpoint to the eerie calm of her sea-green eyes. The bridge displays painted chaotic patterns of crimson and gold - the fiery aftermath of yet another attack on the battered frigate. 

Explosive shockwaves rippled outward in brutal ripples, hammering the hull even as thermal radiation scorched the external shields. Acrid smoke eddied through the compartment along with an acrid tang of ozone and scorched materials. Alarms screamed discordantly, drowning out cries of pain from wounded crewmen drifting away unconscious or moaning from stations.

"Hold on, people!" Astrid's voice cut above the din with icy clarity despite the adrenaline coursing through her veins. Metal straining against her gloved hand, she spared a glimpse of Commander Mercer. Sweat gleamed on his brow beneath the short-cropped chestnut hair, and his grip was white-knuckled on the helm yoke. Yet his gaze remained fixed intently ahead, exuding steady resolve even in the face of such overwhelming violence.

Leo was already plotting their move even as the pulsar's fury peaked. He threw the ship into a stomach-churning corkscrew dive, evading one burst of gamma radiation by mere kilometers. At the same time, the Marauder's internal compensators worked overtime to mute the effects of acceleration that would tear less robust vessels apart. The relentless G-forces kept Vex's seat harness taut against her torso, the pressure forcing air from her lungs in ragged gasps.

"The distortion..." Leo panted, gritting his teeth. Their makeshift patch couldn't hold up indefinitely against stresses like this. "...is slipping! We can't bank too many more turns without— "

A massive jolt struck the frigate out of nowhere, slamming both officers forward against their restraints. More than half of the main viewscreen blew out in a hail of sparks as the impact overloaded nearby conduits and sent searing coolant leaking across bulkheads. Frantic red lights strobed against bloodshot eyes, casting the bridge into a surreal hellish twilight.

Leo turned to survey the damage, countenance grim with concern for his new crewmates. A dozen personnel lay slumped in chairs or on the deck plates where they'd been hurled, some dazed but breathing, others ominously still amidst spreading puddles of dark fluid. Emergency response drones buzzed past him, dispensing medi packs and triaging the worst casualties.

Meanwhile, Astria's fierce glare riveted on the last functioning display, showing the looming shape of something big and menacing approaching fast. An upgraded variant of the Empire's prized Interdictor class cruiser, no doubt fresh from dockyards humming with eager speculation about their fateful pursuit. Even crippled, the Marauder still posed a formidable threat; but odds tipped heavily the other way now.

"We've got company," Astra reported curtly, pointing to the dreadnought's shadowy prow. "I don't know how much longer we can keep dancing. Our shields are on the brink, and—"

Another explosion rocked the derelict hulk of their erstwhile escorts, fragments spinning through space to ricochet off the Marauder's already scarred flank. Whatever was inside had detonated like a small asteroid, sending debris lancing through the void in a deadly cloud.

"We have to make our run NOW!" Leo emphasized urgently.

Leo grabbed one of the crash webbing restraints, hastily looping it under the ribcage of a critically injured ensign even as nearby alarms shrieked at earsplitting volume and hydraulic servo-whines reverberated through metal decks. Across the cockpit, Vex stared fixedly at the main display, her expression etched in lines of cold desperation as she rapidly recalculated their dwindling prospects against the onrushing shadow of their pursuer.

"NOW, you say?" Her voice dripped sarcasm and barely concealed ire. "Great, thanks for the vote of confidence." Vex whirled around, eyes wild flecks of emerald in a face smudged with engine soot and streaked with nervous perspiration that made her auburn hair stick close to her skull. "You're telling me this is a good time to— Holy expletive!"

A near miss sent a shockwave through the hull, nearly toppling Leo as he struggled to secure the wounded officer's harness. Pulsating red warnings flooded his augmented vision: main power conduits failing, critical coolant leaks, internal fires raging unchecked...

"What...happened?" Vex snarled over the wail of warning klaxons. "You said these derelicts were empty! Where'd they get the goddamn ordnance?"

"I...don't know." Leo spat out the words as he heaved the ensign into place with numbing precision born of repetition and horror. "May have been automated remote triggers...or someone boarded them beforehand..." He straightened slowly, feeling the press of high-G forces beginning their relentless squeeze. "...doesn't matter now. We gotta move."

With a snarl of impatience, Vex lunged across the cabin toward the helm, her long black sleeves flapping like tattered wings. "Like hell we do, Hotshot. This tub's falling apart on its own!" She seized control of the grav compensators, slamming the manual override levers forward - a desperate bid to outrun the impending apocalypse before their craft disintegrated into scrap.

"Hold on!" Even as warning sirens screamed into unbearable cacophony and flickering holographic displays spun dizzying false vistas of reality, Vex locked eyes with Leo, her gaze unflinching despite the chaotic bedlam engulfing them both. Her voice was a low growl, fueled by adrenaline and sheer force of will...and something darker, something that spoke of untold losses and buried grief. 

"We'll take our chances with the unknown over turning into Space junk anytime. Now if you ain't busy playing hero..."

The last word dissolved into a roar of escaping atmosphere, the scream of stresses bearing down on stressed shipmetal to catastrophic effect... and yet amid all the fury and chaos, there lingered a moment of fragile understanding passing between two warriors forged in very different crucibles - a silent acknowledgement of each other’s strength and resilience under pressure, coupled with an instinctual distrust honed by countless near-death experiences.

They would need every ounce of both...

✦ ✦ ✦

The grav compensators roared like beasts unleashed as Vex slammed the manual override levers into their stops - a reckless, frantic attempt to salvage any semblance of control even as their battered frigate hemorrhaged atmosphere into the void. The violent decompression shriek ripped through the decks, a deafening wail that seared ears and splintered bones alike until it became just another grating accompaniment to their perilous pirouettes through the debris field.

"CHICKENS OUT?" Leo bellowed somehow above the cacophonous din, his face contorted into a snarl, eyes wide with adrenalized terror yet burning with unwavering defiance. Even as he clung for dear life to the shaking consoles, desperately trying to stabilize their flight path, he shot Vex a sidelong glare steeped in equal parts challenge, incredulity and - damned if he could deny it - raw, unreasoning fear. 

Because they were all but naked facing annihilation now, strapped in alongside spent hardware while metal howled and blistered around them, the cold dark abyss pressing ominously closer with each passing second...and still there she stood, this feral corsair who'd dragged him screaming back from the jaws of oblivion no less than twice already, utterly unmoved by the prospect of perishing horribly amidst the wreckage of their derelict charge vessel.

"FUCK YOU," Vex snarled back at a pitch that shook offal from the ceiling tiles without anyone noticing. Though for once her anger was tempered by a grim sort of admiration for the man who dared defy her even when they teetered on the brink of death together. Because who else but some reckless son-of-a-bitch test pilot from the dawn of the star age would have the stones to throw defiance at Vex Ross while Hell itself rained infernal retribution upon their heads?

And so, as the frigates twisted and bucked erratically beneath them, their hull plating groaning in protest against the brutal shocks of orbital burns and atmospheric scours, neither commanding officer spared a thought for mercy or surrender...

Leo slapped the inertial dampeners back online after what felt like an eternity of stomach-churning freefall and the Starlight Marauder sluggishly righted herself - though whether from skill or dumb luck remained unclear, given the numberless system faults now flashing red in her console banks.

"GRIM TALENT," he conceded hoarsely, acknowledging Vex's masterful seamanship and reflexive aptitude under stress with a nod of begrudging respect, his mouth set in a thin line as shock began to set in amidst the raging storm of emotions that followed the adrenaline crash...

"We're headed for a bad date with Neptune's magnetosphere in ten," intoned the shipboard AI in its customary detached monotone, the metallic lilt cutting through the relative calm with unsettling clarity. An eerie silence followed, punctuated only by the creaks and scrapes of scorched metal as damaged panels protested the continued buffeting from residual solar winds.

Neither commander dwelled much on the looming catastrophe, far too occupied dealing with aftermath of their narrow escape - and themselves. For though the external threats had receded somewhat for the moment, the danger zone really lay within as feelings once contained now threatened to boil over...

Leo turned slowly to regard Vex, watching her face in profile illuminated faintly by flickering screens while she wrestled the helm, a mask of icy concentration barely masking the tumult swirling behind those ice-blue eyes. There in that singular glimpse, something clicked, the unspoken understanding passing between battle-hardened comrades-inarm snapped into crystal clarity.

✦ ✦ ✦

Commander Leo Mercer squinted against the harsh blue-white glare, gaze tracing the tortured wisps of superheated plasma ion-tails thrashing through the void like celestial whips across their stern wake. As starboard consoles spat smoke and crackles of overloaded circuitry, Vex held the battered craft steady with a viselike grip, her knuckles white beneath the chitinous exosuit.

"Had enough fun for today?" taunted Mercer, still riding the delayed thrill, though acrid sweat stung his brow. Even in crisis, this Corsair bitch knew how to play dirty, dancing them just beyond the precipice before yanking the rope - his stomach somersaulted anew recalling the death-defying gravity deflection maneuver. The raw intensity of Vex and her ship's performance had left him awestruck despite himself.

"GRIM TALENT," concurred Vex dryly without looking back, acknowledging his begrudging approval of her mettle with a subtle curl of defiance at the corner of lips, painted scarlet red a stark contrast against her pale skin beneath the utilitarian silver armor plating. In that charged, breathless silence broken only by dying sparks and protesting ship's hydraulics, Mercer knew they'd shared something profound, fleeting but unforgettable as it passed like a comet's tail blazing across black sky.

The metallic tone cut through the fragile truce with unnerving abruptness. "Estimated collision vector with Neptune's magnetosphere in thirteen minutes and counting."

An eerie stillness descended over the bridge as reality reared up in cold, unforgiving clarity. A mere fraction of light-speed separation, mere seconds given the behemoth planet's colossal mass, yet sufficient to crush the Starlight like a tin can should their trajectory not correct. The ship lurched again, scraping against a stray asteroid with teeth-gritting violence, hull plating shedding flakes of scorching heat-treated aluminum.

Avalanches of emotions tumbled free, mercifully muted beneath the din of warnings wailing and warning klaxons screaming frantic alerts, but there could be no denying what transpired - a seismic shift between commanders. That final brush with mortality had torn aside masks of bravado, revealed the raw pulsing center of each beneath. The air throbbed with palpable electricity as both captains absorbed this new truth, neither bothering to hide knowing glances that sparkled with wary appreciation tinged with unmistakable... attraction?

Yet Mercer made no move to intervene, allowing Vex full control, respecting her mastery, trusting instinctively. In her hands alone lay their salvation as perilous and daunting as any enemy battleship - one small miscalculation or hesitation could spell disaster.

Neptune loomed larger ahead with each second, its majestic ring system shimmering like shattered diamonds sprinkling the black velvet between them, a breathtaking but terrifying sight. Merciless gravitational forces beckoned them towards destruction like a lovesick Romeo rushing headlong into a moonlit Juliet's arms never noticing the poison dagger at her throat.

This was no game, no training exercise or carefully controlled experiment. This was real, happening here, happening now... their lives hung in the balance on the razor-edged hinge of fate, balanced in some cosmic dice roll unseen.

Leo swallowed dryly against the rising tide of anxiety, ears ringing, pulse thundering in sync with the ship's klaxon screams. At long last, a sense crept over him, whisper-thin and undeniable - hope. Hope they would make it out whole, alive. And perhaps...

Perhaps not alone anymore either.
