# Chapter 8: The Garden of Nightshades

Revelation came not a moment too soon—their cloaked entwinement broke apart as Aurelia twisted sharply out of Caelum's grasp, whirling with knife-in-hand to face her interloper. "Stand down, witch," she commanded coolly, her voice dripping ice despite the heavy thud-thudding of her heart against ribs fever-slick beneath her damp dress. Water dripped from the foliage above, striking the stone path to splatter tiny jewels across the ancient slabs. 

Rain pelted down in earnest now, reducing visibility to little more than fragmented shapes beneath the canopy of nightshades. But there was no mistaking the venomous glint in Aurora's eyes—Aurelia could practically taste the hate radiating off the usurping priestess of Morrigan. Behind her, Caelum tensively flexed the blade sheathed at his hip, yet made no overt move towards their assailant, conserving stealth and leverage for an optimum strike. Or so it seemed.

"How nice of you to drop by, Mother dearest," Aurora hissed around white, thin lips. A slender dirk appeared in her hand, its moon-cut blade dancing eerily in the dim illumination as if mesmerized by its own deadly potential. "I've been waiting to give my dear child the special blessing of death—and perhaps collect what's owed me by that spineless fool of a king for desertion during the war."

"Oh, I'm shaking in my boots," Aurelia scoffed, even as instinct urged her to be wary of Aurora's ruthless, fanatical tendencies. Rain soaked through her dress, rendering the slippery folds an unwieldy burden, but the chilly droplets seeping beneath her blouse heightened the electric awareness prickling her senses. Her gaze flickered toward Caelum—a hint of reassurance, perhaps a silent promise of shared survival amidst this fresh peril? Their eyes locked, mere inches separating them, silently charting unspoken battles to come.

With uncanny grace defying her soaked garments, Aurora launched forwards in a blur of sinuous motion, flashing steel aimed dead center at Aurelia's chest. At the same instant, a shadow detached swiftly from the treeline behind the alchemist. Caelum moved with inhuman speed to flank their attacker, snatching her wrist mid-lunge and wrenching, shattering bone with a sickening crunch. The dirk clattered uselessly to the wet stones as Aurora shrieked and writhed, clutching her ruined limb. The blindfolded prince seized the fleeting opportunity, yanking the struggling priestess backwards against him as a human shield, using her pliant form to protect his vulnerable belly from retaliatory blades—

"Enough games!" Aurelia barked, pivoting within Caelum's encircling embrace to thrust her own dagger pointedly in Aurora's direction, pressing the cold steel against the smooth column of the other woman's throat. Caelum's muscular arms tightened around her, shielding her flank as they turned in tandem to face Aurora anew—an exquisite tango of deadly dominance tempered by fragile trust between predator and prey. Distantly, an owl screeched its mournful cry in the night sky overhead, punctuating the tense stalemate between killers as the storm raged on around them, oblivious to the bloodshed it harbored. For now, at least, life—or something akin to it—hung in precarious balance...

Sweat dripped relentlessly down Aurelia's spine as the weight of Prince Caelum's hard body pressed inexorably into hers, forcing her chest to flatten against his armored back plate. The knife at Aurora's throat gleamed silver under the tempest-tossed moonlight as the alchemist maintained her merciless grip, her finger tightening infinitesimally should the captive twitch towards betrayal. 

"Silence, wretched thing," Aurelia hissed through gritted teeth, the acrid tang of fear mingling unpleasantly with the heady musk of man sweat that permeated the Prince's clothing. Her left hand hovered protectively near her vial-filled belt pouch even as she braced herself to fend off any reckless counterattack.

Slowly, grudgingly, the priestess's head lolled sideways, accepting defeat for now. Yet Aurelia knew better than to underestimate an enemy cornered and thus rendered all the more deadly. Aurora's gaze darted past the blade to narrow behind Aurelia, meeting Caelum's dark stare reflected there with a calculating intensity that made flesh crawl beneath armor and fine silk alike. Any moment, the scent of ozone might herald their foe's desperate charge...but then again, such was not the rebellious warrior-bitch's style. Where treachery lurked, subtlety often followed close at heel like a faithful hound.

"I could kill you where you stand, witch-crone." Caelum's low-pitched murmur stirred warm air along Aurelia's upper lips, his breath uneven and hot against the sensitive skin of her forehead where he cradled her jaw roughly in his palm. His other arm remained coiled tight across her breasts, an unyielding barier of solid muscle wrapped in supple leather, anchoring her firmly against his chest.

"And perhaps I'd let you try," Aurelia retorted, her voice coming out almost breathless despite the effort to sound composed. Beneath the layers of woven wool and gossamer linen, the rapid flutter of Caelum's heart pulsed syncopated rhythms against her own as if their two wills—and bodies—shared a primal, electric intimacy forged in the crucible of danger.

Abruptly, the storm intensified around them—the howl of wind doubling in ferocity until it became a deafening roar seeping through cracks in the citadel battlements. Rain came crashing down like a thousand hammer-blows, pounding the landscape with savage force, threatening to scour away all footpaths and hiding places. It painted streaks of mud and muck on Caelum's armor, streaking his bare cheeks a mask of earthy stains—so achingly real for Aurelia that she ached to savor each cool rivulet on her tongue as he gasped and panted heavily, clearly battling the urge to shed the heavy leather confines.

"What's... wrong..." Caelum gritted out the words, straining audibly under the deluge, his hands flexing as if torn between wanting to draw his sword, cast both ladies aside to flee, or press closer, mold Aurelia fully into the contours of his own desperate desire until they were naught but one entity riding out the maelstrom.

"It's nothing," Aurelia snapped back shortly though adrenaline surged wildly in her veins. She sensed it in every tensing muscle, in that telltale accelerated cadence thrumming through the chambers beneath his ribs—her mortal enemy, the man who wanted her dead, also being her fiercest...lover? Ally? Neither quite fit.

Aurelia tugged at Caelum's arm, trying to urge him forward and sheltered behind a crumbling, rain-worn stone wall. Her voice was low and urgent, almost pleading despite her best efforts to sound commanding. 

"Hurry, before you become too saturated to move!" The words slipped out unbidden, betraying her own trepidation. With the violent tempest tearing down upon them, this close proximity to death and madness seemed ever more precarious.

Caelum stumbled a step forward, blindly guided by her pulling hand. His movements grew clumsy, limbs heavy and uncoordinated, as if the waterlogged armor and sodden clothing worked against natural responses honed by years of training in martial prowess. He nearly collided with the ancient wall, head snapping up to focus on their route while Aurelia quickly sidestepped ahead to ensure passage unhindered.

In the darkness of night, enshrouded further by driving sheets of rain and veils of mist, sounds took on eerie proportions. Each crackle of lightning illuminated vast expanses of landscape reduced to primal chaos. Howling gusts whipped through skeletal boughs, shredding leaves into an aerial ballet. And between these flashes, shadows loomed ominous - grasping, beckoning.

The executioner's fingers curled tighter around Aurelia's wrist even as she led onward, reflexively seeking to anchor himself lest the raging torrent sweep him away. Though drenched and shivering, there was no doubt about the hard urgency pressed unerringly against her middle. Something primal compelled him forward, heedless of any perils that might await.

In spite of herself, a thrill raced down Aurelia's spine at the intense embrace—and simultaneously her palms began to sweat within gloves, muscles seizing as a wave of uncertainty crashed over her. Could she really trust her greatest foe? The man who stalked her from inky recesses of despair, exulting in every tremor of fear?

The rain drummed ceaselessly along the stones as they navigated deeper into the garden. Moldering statuary glinted faintly in morbid beauty, while fountains groaned, spewing forth torrents that splashed violently across moss-slick paths, creating mini-turbulence to contend with.

Suddenly, an especially blinding bolt of lightning tore across indigo skies - and with its brilliant illumination came the horror of seeing, stark and clear-eyed, the stark need etched across Prince Caelum's face like bas-relief modeling on marble. For a heartbeat, his visage emerged lit within shades of silver, all other features overshadowed by those eyes—eyes burning, ablaze with a hunger the storm couldn't match.

In that fleeting instant, captured forever within her mind's eye, Aurelia glimpsed not just the desperation clawing at the Prince; she saw the seething turmoil that drove him to this pitch of obsession - that drew him inexorably to her own tumultuous soul forged in the crucible of poison and vengeance. A symphony of contrasts, so opposite yet intertwined: light vs. shadow, innocence lost vs. corrupted purity...

No sooner had understanding dawned within her than the world plunged back to deep ink-blue obscurity, light and shadows swirling into dizzying confusion once more. Caelum's ragged breathing and the rapid tattoo of his heart pulsed against Aurelia as surely as her own did within her chest—that relentless cadence she'd grown attuned to, the metronome marking every shared crisis, triumph, and darkest secret.

And then—

With brutal suddenness, his fingers tightened to the point of bruising pain atop her own.

The storm ravaged on, shattering the already precarious structure of consciousness and plunging them both further into madness. Flickers of lightning illuminated their faces, revealing contorted anguish, raw desire, and unmasked depravity. Their bodies entwined as one, slick with rain and copious tears, writhing against the jagged stone wall.

Aurelia felt herself splintering under the ruthless grip of Caelum's fingers, each cruel pressure urging forth the secrets she dared not confess aloud. It was as though he peeled away her layers, anatomizing her very soul until only the truth remained.

"I do not... I will-" She gasped, shredded vocals betraying the turmoil ravaging her mind. Tears mingled with rain on her cheeks as she strained against his hold, grappling for any remnant of control.

"You can't evade me," he groaned, teeth bared in animalistic fervor. "I taste your surrender even as you cling to it. Every time I bind you, claim you, our threads grow tighter. Until soon there will be no discernible difference between us. We'll bleed the same color behind this mask—" His words dissolved into guttural moans as he rocked against her, hands sliding beneath her skirt to seize her dripping sex.

Their coupling seemed to transcend corporeal limits, becoming a pagan rite where blood, bone, and sinew tangled. Aurelia keened, overwhelmed by pleasure bordering into agony, sensation bleeding into delirium. Caelum thrust into her anew, ferocious and merciless, pounding her body into resonance with his unhinged rhythm.

In the throes of climax, Aurelia's scream tore free, a shattered howl born from hell itself. Her inner walls clenched viciously around the raging hardness spearing her open, milking his release as rivers of his seed coursed forth to paint her walls in slick, glistening trails.

As the aftershocks dissipated, leaving them limp and tangled, a strange stillness settled over them—the anticipation before a breaking storm. Caelum withdrew slowly, easing Aurelia onto the damp earth, but kept his arms locked around her as if he feared she might vanish without warning.

Then a rasping laughter bubbled up, almost hysterical, from the Prince's lips—the mocking gleam rekindling in his hooded gaze.

"Do you believe it now?" he whispered, voice husky with something that sounded perilously close to contentment. "Even a blind man can see which way the threads are woven. You cannot run, Aurelia. You belong to me, flesh and spirit, and I shall ensure neither part ever escapes my grasp..."

He trailed off, lost in thought, then looked up sharply at some unseen sound within the gardens. A new tension etched his features—a predator poised on the brink of springing. Aurelia too heard the night awakening; a chilling chorus of scuttlings and soft whispers crept closer with unnerving stealth.

A twig snapped ominously nearby, and both turned their gaze toward the source of the disturbance. Something watched them from the darkness, unseen eyes fixed upon the drenched, entwined figures. A moment stretched taut and unbearable before—

"Aurelia. Move." Caelum's urgent command whipped past her ear. With superhuman strength, he hauled her to her feet and shoved her behind a crumbling statue barely standing sentinel against the tide of chaos erupting behind them. Across the garden, silhouettes burst from the shadows—the outlines of armed men, hunting parties, descending upon their position.
