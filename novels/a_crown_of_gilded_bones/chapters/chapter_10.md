# Chapter 10: The Midnight Masquerade

Cold metal dug cruelly into the tender flesh of Aurelia's inner thigh through layers of silken dress. She bit back a whimper as the guard's grip tightened on her, holding her pinned with effortless strength that spoke of years honed in servitude to their brutal masters. Steel flashed anew, the soldiers' weapons slicing the thick air above with sharp menace. 

Aurelia fixed her gaze past them, training it on the intricately engraved door looming behind the patrol. That entrance promised safety, seclusion from the prying eyes—and iron-fisted control—of the royal family and their ilk. All she needed was time to slip through its stately barrier unseen, then find sanctuary within the winding, labyrinthine passages of the palace deeps. 

Time she did not have.

"Why hide?" one of the guard asked, voice dripping malice as he ground the sword against a nearby flagstone. "The hunt continues while you cower like prey." His hand shifted to wrap around Aurelia's slender wrist in a grip designed to crush beneath its unyielding pressure if necessary. "Perhaps you underestimate the wrath of the crown."

A shiver cascaded down her spine at the subtle threat lacing his words. This close, the acrid tang of sweat, leather, and raw steel hung heavy between them, mingling with the heady elixir still lingering on her tongue where Caelum had claimed her earlier. Memories of his teeth, hot and demanding, assaulted her senses even in the midst of such danger.

Her head jerked upon its slender neck to meet the soldier's gaze, lips parting over eager breaths that quickened further under the predatory intensity smoldering in those pale orbs so like ice chips. One flicker of movement, one wrong feint, and they would end her life with swift efficiency, leaving naught but a crimson puddle and whispers of her treachery.

Yet even knowing these risks, desire burned hotter than ever within her bloodstream—an inconvenient, pulsing ache born of long nights spent fantasizing about an impossible conquest to quench her thirst for male supremacy, raw power, and carnal domination...the very attributes currently embodied by the man towering before her, dark robes billowing as he braced himself to strike.

Then, in a flash of blinding silver, the executioner's blade soared toward her neck with surgical precision—the same merciless edge that had driven home again and again through human bodies until she succumbed to the sweetest oblivion. Panic seized her lungs, cutting off air; for a moment she was lost in visions of Caelum's callous hands bathing hers in her victim's lifeblood—

CRASH! 

A wild mallet blow resounded through the chamber, sending debris scattering across the ornate floor tiles. Shouts erupted in response, rising in chaotic crescendo as panic rippled outward like water from a thrown stone. The soldier gripping Aurelia staggered forward reflexively, off-balance.

In the span of a single, thunderous heartbeat, she spun from his grasp, whirling to flee...only to collide directly into a muscular chest clad in supple black leather armor adorned with ornate midnight blue brocade embroidered in molten gold thread. Strong yet warm arms instantly encircled her waist, hauling her flush against his solid form with a primal growl...

And there amidst the bedlam unfolding around them, amid crashing masonry and splintering wood, she realized with electric shock that Caelum wore no blindfold tonight. Eyes blazing like frozen stars, he claimed her mouth ruthlessly in a desperate, bruising kiss that left her reeling and clutching desperately at the ebony curls spring.

The world tilted on its axis, shattering around Aurelia into a kaleidoscope of shattered marble and screaming shadows. All rational thought fled as she recoiled wildly, skin crawling, from the sight of Caelum's eyes blazing beneath hooded lids, their starlight pale save where pupils dilated as if siphoning pitch-dark inlustre. Lips crashed together in desperate violence, the kiss bruising deeper than any blade could cut as she clutched his dark curls, nails biting into skin that would never feel foreign again even buried beneath silk gloves.

She felt each flex of his arm banded tight around her, fingers spread wide enough to leave bruises—imprisoning her, anchoring her in place as his teeth tore at her lower lip, the sharp sting searing right down to blood's first crimson whispers. Time itself became distorted, stretched thin as stretched rubber while his tongue invaded plunderingly, demanding a reckoning from all tender flesh it brushed. Pressure built low in her belly as if an arrowhead of lust pierced ever deeper, winding tightly in unmarked spiral of need born anew every time he touched her—a caress, a glance, a breath hot against ear shell, igniting an inferno too vast to comprehend.

Just when sanity began teetering on crumbling precipice, he wrenched away with a snarl whose guttural edges sent tremors licking up spine and calves clenched like iron vice, threatening to buckle under her own trembling. Panting harshly, Aurela pulled back only far enough to glare defiant up at Caelum's face—diced ice masking storm-riven seas beneath those cold star-gleams.

"You," she spat in tone so low and poisonous, frost might've encrusted lips uttering the words. "Here." One gloved hand lifted, stabbing accusing finger into his chest. "Foolish dare. You seek to play game of thrones amidst pyre of broken stones? Delight yourself in twisted dance amidst chaos?"

His jaw clenched, teeth ground audibly before reply, each consonant clipped cruelly: "I am prince, not pawn, little viper. My 'game' sees beyond court intrigue—deals in higher stakes."

Stung by his mocking appellation, she hissed air between clenched teeth—"Hapless fool—!" Cut short by a thunderous crash from within hall as another support beam gave way. Screams now turned frantic wails, signaling a new wave of near-panic sweeping palace, heralding impending collapse long deferred. Through veil of sweat-dampened hair flung across narrow brow and narrowed gaze fixed almost lovingly upon her contorted face, Caelum watched her like hawk studying wounded rabbit...considering whether to dispatch final deathstroke now and put end to agony of her fear. Waiting mere heartbeats.

When at last Caelum spoke again, voice dropped to deadly purr—the sound of soft darkness lapping at shorelines: "This masquerade may yet prove a pleasure after all, should we choose to embrace this fire instead of fleeing its flames." His gloved palm stroked reverently across her cheekbone, fingertips tracing curve of petrified muscles.

Aurelia's eyes snapped shut as if against blade's thrust, lips parting minutely to catch ragged hitch of breath at the intimate contact. When fingers then wrapped tight around her wrist, yanking her close once more, she gasped outright, heart hammering with frantic desperation, each beat thrumming urgent warning.

✦ ✦ ✦

Caelum's hand gripped her wrist like iron links, wrenching Aurelia forward until she bumped unceremoniously against the stone wall, barely able to resist his forceful drag due to the fetters binding her ankles. She staggered slightly at the abrupt impact, shoulder blades denting into aged brickwork as she sucked in desperate lungfulls of dusty air.

Frantic heartbeat reverberated through her chest, drumfire pounding in time with panic that threatened to overwhelm all rational thought. Tremors coursed down both arms as she flailed weakly at bindings securing her wrists within the velvet restraints, claws scrabbling futilely against plush as she writhed for purchase against the unforgiving prison. Eyes wide open now in full-blown flight reflex, they bulged feverishly beneath lowered lashes as she strained to meet the obsidian mask, brain desperately searching out telltale signs of impending violence amidst the blindfold's concealing shadows. Every muscle straining taut as bowstring, tense anticipation hung heavy as leaden stormclouds overhead, poised to unleash chaotic tempests.

Still holding her fast, Caelum brought his other hand up to wrap around the opposite bicep, effectively pinning her arm against side of her head with his own. Lean body pinned her back against the cold stone, an impenetrable barrier. A low hum resonated just beyond her ear, throat vibrating in rhythm with harsh inhalation of winded breath. Then that deep, menacing chuckle rippled through him, seeming to emanate from very depths of his being—a mirth devoid of warmth or human comfort.

"Scream for me." Breathed, hot and feral against chilled skin, teeth grazing sensitized curves in cruel encouragement. "Let them hear you. No true prisoner holds silence so well...unless they're broken already."

Fear's icy tendrils wormed their way into every vulnerable crevice as numbness bloomed, creeping paralysis seeping inexorably up from toes toward the pit of her stomach. Panic-induced slick coats her inner thighs while every nerve shudders under assault, trembling on knife's edge between breaking completely or fighting on defiantly.

Aurelia bit her lip hard enough to draw copper warmth across the white expanse, tears pricking eyes grown hazy with terror. Jaw set taut, she glared at the mask, a silent rebuke at its concealment even as some twisted dark facet within herself responded fiercely to the power exerted over her—hungering to be mastered and owned absolutely, to shed skin old as yesterday's skin and emerge reborn from inferno these unspoken words ignited. But such madness could never birth from place of ratiocination alone; primal forces awakened, responding to ancient tribal drums beating at core of psyche.

So small, fragile, utterly helpless amidst crushing oppression. Vulnerability exposed in all its brutal candor, stripped to threads of raw emotion bared before merciless predator's piercing gaze. And he reveled in it - the power trip writ clear on the lewd smile curled about crimson lips.

"Now beg, princess," husky taunt breathed softer than a lover's promise only to pierce eardrums with volume of demon's roar. "Beg me to stop violating your precious privacy with my filthy imagination."

Air caught audibly in throat as shock locked her rigid, leaving only trembling ruin in its wake. Unmoored from every anchoring tenet of moral decency and dignity, Aurelia's mind teetered giddily at precipice's edge where shame, submission, and something indistinct but infinitely darker entwined in unholy matrimony.

✦ ✦ ✦

The room quivered around them like thunder-tossed pages—their volatile interplay shaking the very foundations. Eyes still glazed by the haze of a thousand sordid whispers, Aurelia felt raw and ravaged, flayed open without mercy, every bruise and scrape laid bare for a masterful opponent to dissect.

"I-" The word jammed half-formed in her shredded voice box as if coated in molten lead and scraping against the roof of her mouth en route to freedom. Caelum's cruel satisfaction resonated through every pore, fueling her humiliation into an inferno capable of torching reason and propriety alike.

Beneath his unyielding scrutiny, Aurelia's gaze flickered helplessly to avoid engaging with the depth of sadistic pleasure etched across those chiseled features. This man would own her soul, carve out every last vestige of humanity until nothing remained but worship—a twisted altar forged from ash and despair upon which she'd offer up her pitiful existence for scraps of validation.

"You...will not..." She dragged each broken syllable like shards of glass across vocal cords, a desperate plea more worthy of a begging thief than the rightful heir to Vethari's crown. "I am Queen—"

"Calm yourself, my regal viper," he drawled, a silk handkerchief appearing out of nowhere to dab at the corners of his mouth, wiping away the tangy remnants of her defiance. "Your majesty titles mean less than rotting entrails here, where one rule reigns supreme: mine." His smile turned venomous as gleaming fangs punctuated a low, derisive chuckle. "And I've grown rather bored with your futile protests. In fact..."

He trailed off purposefully, his weight shifting forward to hover mere inches from her ear as the mask's black void closed in around their tumultuous world once more, eclipsing all light. The temperature dropped precipitously in those confines as frozen dread seeped into her marrow—Aurelia's breath caught in a strangled sob as her thoughts unraveled beneath the tidal wave of impending doom.

Caelum's next spoken words shattered the fragile silence: "Time we stepped out, Your Radiance...I trust you comprehend the stakes?" The silky undertones painted an entirely different picture—a lurid tapestry rich with the thread of mortal sacrifice woven seamlessly into the fabric of dark indulgence. "A little bird confided my intentions to one most...unfortunate. Should I falter even a hair's breadth, fate decrees our joined shadows will stain the streets with blood. Yours, mine, the night's—all forever matted as one foul mess." He leaned back enough to part the mask's seam, his eyes twin obsidian mirrors reflecting a darkness so profound it seemed capable of swallowing stars whole. 

"How intriguing," he mused aloud as if voicing an afterthought, a wicked grin twisting those elegant features. "To surrender completely now and suffer an agonizing death for naught—oh what a delicious torment." The corners of his mouth pulled upward into a cruel mockery of a smile, razor-fine daggers honed solely for carving her soul apart piece by excruciating piece. Then his voice softened, slipping in oily tendrils under her guard, coiling around heartstrings like a snake preparing to strike:

"On the other hand...should you succumb gracefully? Yield yourself utterly and without reservation to the whims of your Master...perhaps, just perhaps, there might be hope for sweet oblivion in a way none of us can currently fathom."
