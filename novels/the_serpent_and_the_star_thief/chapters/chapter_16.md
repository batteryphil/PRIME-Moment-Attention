# Chapter 16: Chapter 16

With quick, purposeful movements, Caelum knelt to secure his boots, black leather polished to mirror finish by his diligent hands. Each buckle clicked shut with finality. Aurelia remained prone, immobile except for the steady rise and fall of her chest as she fought to regulate her thunderous heartbeat. She could still feel him, warmth seeping into the core of her being even as his substantial form was withdrawn, replaced only by lingering impressions of flesh meeting flesh. When no answer came, Caelum straightened, his profile sharp against the candlelit gloom.

"Aurelia," he repeated under his breath, each syllable weighted with subtle danger. His gaze swept the crumpled bedclothes before settling on her upturned face, features still locked in serene composure despite the tempest raging within her psyche's depths. With measured steps, one cloaked in quiet command, he approached until standing just beside the bed. Too close, really—their bodies almost touched, drawing her attention like a moth to flame.

She noted minutely how his breath hitched upon inhaling; a soft exhalation escaping unguarded lips carrying a faint tang of cloves, hinting at secrets held close. No sound issued forth that might betray their tryst—a miracle given the riotous clamor of senses presently consuming her awareness. Awareness narrowed, pinpricks of focus honing in on Caelum until he became her entire world—eyes masked yet burning with intensity, body poised in contained elegance reflecting sharpened instincts honed past mastery. What new schemes swirled behind those obsidian folds? Were they connected somehow to the King's precarious situation?

All conjecture stalled as calloused fingers landed with feather-light precision upon her parted lips. Shock rippling through her system, Aurelia jerked, attempting to break free from the hypnotic spell woven by his presence. But Caelum gently grasped her nape, fingers flexing ever so slightly to convey restraint rather than dominance—yet an unmistakable warning threaded beneath the delicate caress. It took supreme effort for her to relax beneathhis touch, acknowledging this as a claim, both possessive and protective.

"I did what I promised," she whispered, tone hoarse despite her best efforts to keep it even. "No witnesses survived my... attentions." Steel glimmered behind his mask as he nodded once, acceptance dawning upon the lines of his face. The King lived—and so would theirs be an existence predicated on lies born of necessity.

"The High Chamberlain awaits us," Caelum murmured, releasing her captive jaw with almost wistful finality. Though the motion ended, Aurelia could feel the phantom pressure of his palms against her skin as if branded there. Rising fluidly from the bed, the blindfolded prince pivoted and strode purposefully toward the door, leaving her to trail after, acutely aware of the tangible gulf now stretching between them like an invisible yet insurmountable barrier. They played this game alone, always watching from different sides of the chessboard.

As the door swung open to admit them back into the cloistered castle halls, Aurelia shivered involuntarily. Cooling air kissed her overheated skin, an abrupt contrast to the searing intimacies shared mere minutes prior. Each step carried her closer to the charade they must maintain, the reality of which only served to heighten a visceral, skin-prickling awareness of Caelum now flanking her side. Two halves of a dichotomous whole—venom and justice, darkness and light, predator and prey—inextricably bound by ties of mutual reliance and clandestine desire.

Caelum's boots echoed hollowly down the icy marble corridor, a syncopated counterpoint to the heavy thud of Aurelia's own feet treading behind him. The chill grip of the palace refused to relinquish its grasp, seeping into her very marrow as unyielding as the rigid column of Caelum's spine beside her. His black form seemed to absorb the meager candlelight that struggled to pierce the gloom, creating a stark silhouette that stirred something dark within her breast.

They navigated the labyrinthine halls in silence, the sole sound their footfalls and Aurelia's carefully measured breathing, never daring to release it with any telltale warmth lest her ruse slip. But the quiet proved agonizing, each second straining tighter the cord binding them until she might snap under the strain at any moment. A pulse thrummed between her thighs, a visceral reminder of the fever-pitched clash of desires and denials coursing unseen beneath her layers of deceptions.

As they turned a corner, Caelum halted abruptly, his hand coming up to rest on the hilt of his sword. Aurelia froze, muscles coiled and ready to spring apart at a split-second's notice—but the prince made no move. Instead, he inclined his head minutely toward a tapestry-hung alcove recessed into the wall, where a faint, golden glow emanated from a single burning taper set upon a carved pedestal. A subtle signal, conveyed solely through the slight shift of weight and alteration of posture, transmitting silently the message: Wait. Observe.

Aurelia assimilated the instruction seamlessly, schooled years ago in the art of stalking prey. Now her gaze fell upon the tapestry itself, woven expertly into stylized scenes of battles past—a jarring juxtaposition given her current entente with Caelum. The crimson hue of spilled blood seemed to bleed more vividly in the low light, bathing the area in a sickening flush, almost mimicking the heat kindling within her. She swallowed, tongue darting out to wet suddenly parched lips.

"Do you find pleasure in gloating over death?" 

The question escaped before Aurelia could throttle it back, her tightly leashed frustration finally bursting its seams. Even then, she hardly suspected it was Caelum who responded, voice a low thread woven through the very shadows themselves:

"In my line of duty, one learns to appreciate beauty in the brutal, even amidst the horror." 

His words hung in the sparse air, weighted heavily with veiled meaning neither dared to articulate aloud. Another heartbeat passed, and still Aurelia remained rooted before the barbaric tapestry, transfixed by the way Caelum's profile looked sculpted from the darkness itself—the austere planes etched by the smoldering ember of the solitary flame. And as if drawn by an unseen force, she stepped cautiously forward, placing herself shoulder-to-shoulder with him once more.

Too close; a thousand dagger-points stabbed through her—every fiber screaming warning in a cacophony not easily muffled. Yet she didn't pull away, couldn't bring herself to sever the fragile tether of connection just yet. Perhaps if she leaned hard enough into the magnetism swirling between them, pouring all her pent-up frustration and desire into the narrow space dividing their bodies, some manner of equilibrium could be found...

"Stop torturing yourself..." the prince muttered, the rumble of his voice brushing a feather-light caress across the shell of her ear, barely audible save for those standing closest.

Aurelia shivered at the whispered command, feeling the warm exhalation ghosting across her hypersensitive ear as surely as if fingers had brushed the delicate curve of it. She fought down the urge to turn in his direction, to search out the source of that tantalizing intimacy. Because in doing so, she risked breaching the already precarious walls of restraint holding her together. 

"Easy for you to say," she retorted, her own voice wavering perilously near a whisper despite her best efforts to keep it steady and scornful. Her eyes, glazed a liquid silver by the moonless night, flickered to the right, following the angle of Caelum's obscured face as he stood poised beside her—a looming silhouette cut from the very substance of shadows. Every instinct screamed at her to step back, to create distance between heat-scorched skin and icy resolve. But the promise of that subtle touch lingered, an enticing siren song strumming the strings of her desperation raw.

"Torture?" Aurelia laughed, the sound a brittle thing, more ice crackling than genuine amusement. "Perhaps it's merely your lot in life to watch me unravel, isn't it? Your twisted fascination with the downfall of others never ceases to amaze me." The barest tremor marred the edges of her tone, betraying the tight coil of control within her fraying like overheated rope.

"Calm yourself." This time, Caelum's soft counsel came from precisely where she'd feared it would: mere inches from her temple, his breath cooling the fever-kissed flush painting her cheeks. With agonizing slowness, Aurelia turned her head, profile angled toward him, lips parting as if to defy the implicit order with protest. However—instead of denying him access to her face—the deliberate tilt of her head only served to present herself more fully to his searching inhalation, which slipped past lips, teeth, and tongue to dance along the delicate, blood-warm hollows flanking the column of her throat. 

Her pulse leapt in response, a hectic staccato drumbeat reverberating through every vessel, while the heat radiating off her body mingled with Caelum's cooled exhale to chill and ignite her anew. She squeezed her eyes shut against the onslaught of sensation, a guttural moan gathering in her throat before dying an unspoken death. In this moment, reason fled, replaced by pure, animal hunger. Aurelia knew she teetered on the precipice; one misplaced gesture, a slip into utter abandon, and she would likely never regain purchase. Yet even as trepidation clawed at the corners of her mind, the yearning overwhelming everything else proved too strong to resist.

With a gasp, Aurelia surged forward, crashing a wild, open-mouthed kiss against the pristine mask hiding Caelum's features. Fingers scrambled wildly for purchase as her hips collided with the unyielding bulk of his thigh, the friction deliciously tormenting to her achingly sensitized centers. She devoured at him with a frenzied intensity born of seething need, desperation and ecstasy intermingling until they became indistinguishable from one another. And when their lips finally broke apart, Aurelia sagged against him, chest heaving as she struggled to catch her ragged breath.
