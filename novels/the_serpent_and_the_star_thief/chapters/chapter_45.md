# Chapter 45: Chapter 45

Cold marble dug cruelly into tender flesh where Aurelia collapsed, her cradling arms no shield against the unforgiving floor. Sharp agony lanced up her arm, but distant, insignificant compared to the void gouging away pieces of her soul with each anguished sob. She huddled over Vasilisa's gore-splattered wreckage, barely registering the slickness coating her own limbs—a macabre mosaic of crimson and viscous black.

As reality bleached white around her, one truth alone propelled her limp form forward—to wrap herself completely around this violated husk, bury everything inside the familiar contours until they blended into one desolate creature.

Fingers found her sister's matted locks and tangled deep, searching. Blindly, desperately, frantically, ignoring the jagged edges shredding her own skin. Each futile caress only confirmed what screaming terror already knew—there wouldn't be a gentle stirring or warm pulse emanating from this shredded corpse.

Yet even as madness clawed at the borders of reality, something...shifted. Within the storm-tossed depths of her psyche, the distant thrumming grew louder—a low hum that began as background static before surging to dominate all else. Her heart, still battering the confines of her ribs like a caged animal, developed a queer rhythm attuned to some vast, ethereal cadence.

The infernal brand on her chest burned hotter, throbbing almost painfully between gasping breaths that carried the acrid scent of ozone. Aurelia knew, without looking, that tendrils of shimmering plasma were curling from the sigil's central sigil, tracing the path of her tear-blessed cheeks down the expanse of her torso, leaving glistening rivulets in their wake.

Cleansing, yet corrupting, just like the darkness itself.

In this dizzying pivot, from agonized despair to nascent triumph, time lost meaning. Aurelia stopped tracking hours and moments—a meaningless construct in this burgeoning realm of immortal rebirth. All that existed were the ebb and flow of power, sensations so intense her nerve endings practically screamed, pulsing waves of transformative energy that threatened to consume her whole.

And through it all, a single thought crystallized:

Corvin was next. This exquisite shell housing his royal blood wouldn't last long under Kira and Mal the Butcher's relentless assault. Soon enough, he'd tumble victim to the same unyielding malevolence that had silenced her sisters. His screams would pierce the night like silvered daggers aimed squarely at Aurelia's core. Watching him fight valiantly only to be shredded—broken—and discarded, piece by agonizing piece—all because of what she'd become...

No. Not if she could help it. At any cost, even defiling the purity of the divine flame coursing through her veins, she would not let the prince die as Vasilisa had.

His lips upon hers, a last defiant gesture before the merciful annihilation of eternal sleep—no. That kiss had spoken volumes regarding Corvin's feelings, making Aurelia's resolution absolute.

She would ascend using every ounce of celestial power, but she refused to relinquish her claim on the High Executioner—and with it, his destiny. Their fates, once so hopelessly entwined by mutual pursuit and hatred, became inextricably linked by the sheer scope of Aurelia's emerging might and ambition.

This path chose her, not the other way around. Now, having taken the first irrevocable step toward becoming the dreaded Serpent Empress, there could be no turning back from the hellfire waiting to engulf both Vortex Academy.

The acrid scent of smoke curled up into Aurelia's nostrils, singeing her nasal passages and making them water. Shattered glass crunched beneath her boots as she stood at the edge of the demolished library, its charred remains a grim testament to her newfound strength unleashed upon her enemies. Only moments ago, those ancient tomes had housed secrets ripe for the taking—if she were to endure this chaos. 

But none of the destruction mattered more than preserving Prince Caelum's life. He lay prone behind her, unconscious yet vital, dragged out of the brutal melee by desperate hands. Blood seeped steadily from numerous wounds, staining his white tunic crimson as it pooled faintly around him in the flickering torchlight.

Aurelia felt each drop as if cut from her own flesh—the guilt, the anguish, the primal maternal urge to shield him from all harm. She dared not bend to tend his injuries herself; risking contact would only heighten the perilous pull between them, tempting fate with repercussions dire and delicious. Instead, she focused on constructing a shelter, erecting a makeshift barrier of crates and drapery, praying it would hold against the onslaught long enough for the prince to recover sufficiently to escape.

"You can't keep running forever," came a mocking whisper near her ear, sending shivers down her spine.

She whirled, fist clenched and dripping malice, to face Corvin. Though injured and clearly exhausted, he still exuded an aura of ruthless authority. Behind the mask of blood splatters and grit, his gaze remained fixed unwaveringly upon her, an unsettling intensity burning brighter in the darkness.

"Oh? And what should I do instead, Your Highness?" Aurelia shot back, each word laced with cold venom and contempt. "Kneel before you and plead for mercy while my sisters weep over their butchered bodies?"

"Better than rotting in your own self-imposed purgatory." He took a faltering step closer, pain ravaging his features but determination set in iron. "We're not so different, you and I. Seek to dominate the world...at any cost."

"Spare me your hypocritical moralism," she spat. "At least I don't serve gods who crave human sacrifice!"

"A necessary evil for a higher cause!" Corvin barked, his grip on her upper arm tightening like a vice. A jolt ran through her at the sudden, electrifying contact—a reminder of the potent chemistry crackling just beneath the surface, threatening to detonate into raw, brutal passion whenever they touched. "Unlike your blood magic and twisted obsessions."

"It's not 'blood magic,'" Aurelia hissed, struggling against his grasp with all her might. "It's my birthright! My legacy!" She reveled in the exquisite friction of his fingers digging into her flesh as she wrenched free, the act leaving her skin tinged with bruise marks already forming. It was a small defiance amidst the chaos, worth savoring, even at the cost of further enraging her nemesis.

Caelum chose that moment to groan weakly, drawing both attackers' attention. Aurelia's heart stuttered as Corvin swiftly repositioned himself between the two of them, positioning his limp body like a human shield. She recognized this stance—one meant to intimidate and assert dominance—with dread recognition. Each breath sounded tortured, hinting at his dwindling tolerance.

"So..." Corvin began, his words laced with the menace of a cornered animal, red-rimmed eyes locked onto hers. "Are you going to finish me off now or try to rescue your plaything?"

Aurelia's heart pounded wildly as Corvin turned his wrathful gaze upon her, crouched protectively over Caelum's battered form like a predator about to strike. His rancor dripped from each word, seeping into her veins, setting her nerves alight with anticipatory terror.

"Unhand him," she commanded, every fiber of her being screaming to charge forward and tear Corvin away by force if necessary—but restraint dictated prudence here. If she so much as raised a hand...

Inching closer, she let out a slow exhale, attempting to soften her stance, to show Corvin without speaking that she meant no harm, now that he held her lover as leverage. "Please, Corvin... release him. Let's end this peacefully."

Corvin released a humorless snort, the sound harsh and derisive. "Peacefully?" He raised an eyebrow, incredulity lacing his tone. "Is that what you call it when I've had to endure your sick little games and twisted obsession for years behind these walls? You won't get away with hurting your precious prince, not when I've seen beneath all these—"

He gestured violently, nearly toppling Caelum in the process.

"—pretty lies and painted smiles. Not after the things you do in the dead of night."

The accusation hung heavy in the air, weighted with a cruel accuracy that stole Aurelia's breath. He knew far too much, his intuition cutting deeper than any blade. Her pale cheeks flamed red, the secret of her nocturnal exploits hovering precariously close to exposure.

"D-don't speak of those things," she managed to choke out, trying to keep her voice steady despite the icy dread creeping up her spine. Each heartbeat thundered in her ears, threatening to drown out reason.

"What you don't understand—" she took another cautious step forward, desperation etching her features, every muscle poised for flight or battle—

"what you can never comprehend, Corvin, is how desperately I crave peace after centuries adrift in this unending war within myself!"

Her words burst forth like a pent-up tempest, raw candor and anguish merging into an explosive plea. Tears glazed her eyes, reflecting the flickering torchlight as emotion overwhelmed her control, shredding her usual composed facade in its wake.

"I'm tired of battling these... cravings, these feelings that consume everything else!" Struggling against the torrent of sensation clawing at her senses, she dug her nails into her palms until they bled, the pain focusing fragments of her scattered thoughts. "I need balance, some respite from this endless cycle of hunger and despair, but it terrifies me because..."

Because deep down, she yearned for Caelum's shadowy presence, his blindfolded intensity and whispered darkness.

"I lust after your cold indifference and sterile silence," Aurelia confessed, her voice cracking on the admission.

"Oh God." A trembling sob escaped her lips before she could stifle it. Horror and humiliation warred as she cringed inwardly, realizing how vulnerable she'd become in this moment of unshielded truth.

"Stop... stop speaking such madness, Vex," Corvin demanded grimly, clearly torn between outrage and fascination over her sudden verbal spilling. "There's always been something wrong with you, but even—"

His words chopped abruptly off as Caelum stirred, a low, agonized moan escaping his parted lips. Aurelia rushed to his side, kneeling carefully and brushing back his dark hair to reveal the angry contusions marring his skin.

"Cael—!"

Aurelia's blood-cooled fingers hesitated mere inches from bruised flesh swollen with silent suffering. Her gaze, once piercing emerald, now flickered uncertainly, lost as a ship without anchor amidst tempestuous seas. The admission just spilled had left her dizzy with shame, yet unable—or unwilling—to sever the unspoken threads binding her to her blindfolded companion.

As shock rippled through his tortured form, Caelum stirred again, a low, animalistic groan tearing past clenched teeth. His breathing grew ragged, the rhythmic strain underscoring each labored inhale and stifled exhale. Aurelia's hands moved instinctively, gentling the wayward strands of raven-black locks from bloodied skin etched with livid welts.

With a shuddering gasp, her attention fastened upon the cruel markings marbling his visage—staples, probably, hastily driven home during his fit by desperate, trembling hands. She traced the jagged lines reverently, a maddened whisper falling from her parted lips. "How could I not know? These... he did this."

Her words carried a note of dismay tinged with reluctant awe, an acknowledgment of the savage strength and unyielding ferocity that pulsed beneath Corvin's calculated savagery. As she peered closer, nearly nose to bruise, a frisson of morbid curiosity unfurled within her.

Those cruel marks weren't just testament to brutality; they were a gruesome map of twisted devotion. Each contusion, every stitch, bore vivid testimony to the lengths one might go in pursuit of an unconquerable fixation—much like the suffocating pull keeping Aurelia irrevocably ensnared in her own desires.

For an eternity held captive only in this fleeting instant, there remained no line dividing victor from vanquished—just two broken, blood-stained creatures tethered together via strings of pain and darkest, most forbidden desire.
