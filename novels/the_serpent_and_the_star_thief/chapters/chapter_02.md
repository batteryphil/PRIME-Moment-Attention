# Chapter 2: Chapter 2


Aurelia could not tear herself away, entranced by the hypnotic dance of memories replaying behind Caelum's blindfold: flickering scenes of bloodshed, of life crushed out like spent candles. With each grim tableau, horror trickled down her spine like a serpent's cold saliva. She recoiled, yet his unyielding grip held her fast.

The underground's heavy fug lay thick and clammy against her face, a miasma of aged earth and malevolent energy that clogged her nostrils and clung to her sweat-drenched skin. Disorientation clawed at her mind as the acrid stench of iron and ozone prickled the back of her throat - evidence of the raw, occult forces crackling in her companion's aura.

Caelum's fingers dug into the tender flesh of Aurelia's wrist, leaving crescent-shaped bruises that would bloom like the marks of possession they were. She tried to wrench free, but he pinned her rigidly with his other arm, caging her against the cold, rough-hewn wall. His muscular frame molded to her own like a dark, inhuman shadow come to life.

"P-please..." Aurelia gasped, voice cracking under the relentless pressure of his merciless gaze. "I-I didn't mean...it slipped out..."

Her desperate plea fell on deaf ears, smothered by the oppressive weight of his presence and the damning revelations playing back in his mind - crimes so vile, Aurelia couldn't fathom their full extent. And they'd all been committed while she stood by, mute accomplice to the atrocities wrought under cover of night.

Panic seized her chest, fluttering frantically against the walls of her larynx. Tears sprang hot and unwelcome, scalding trails across her cheeks as her lungs wheezed for a gulp of blessed air denied by the iron vice of Caelum's arms.

The ground convulsed uneasily beneath their feet, ancient stones groaning in protest as arcane energies churned restless depths below. Tiny pillars of dislodged dust swirled aimlessly then settled back into dismal silence, punctuating the ominous vibrations. Aurelia shuddered, knowing these tremors heralded only one thing - the terrible awakening of Arkon's slumbering secrets, and they threatened to consume everything.

But still, Aurelia's eyes remained locked with Caelum's, drawing upon a reservoir of numb resolve that dared confront the dark hunger blazing back at her. Whatever monstrous metamorphosis had taken hold inside him since that fateful day, however much worse the torment he contemplated might prove, she steeled herself to face the unknown, a willing sacrifice should it mean appeasing her torturer and safeguarding any tenuous hope for the future.

Whatever cruel fate awaited her, Aurelia knew the abyss of Caelum's obsessions now yawned wider, swallowing all light and reason in its path. Only time would reveal which of them would finally emerge shattered and broken at the bottom of that void - assuming either survived long enough to get there.

Darkness gathered like quicksilver as they stumbled forward, pressed tightly against Caelum's unyielding chest. Each labored step jarred pain lancing up Aurelia's sides, breaths sharp and harsh in the oppressive quiet. Somewhere ahead, the distant sound of chanting seeped through thin walls, muffled but unmistakable—a grim serenade heralding the terrible ritual on the cusp of completion.

Caelum's grip tightened around Aurelia, his muscles flexing with a coiled strength she both feared and, sickeningly, craved. "What trickery do you employ, witch?" his voice was a low growl, breath hot against the bare skin of her neck where tears carved glistening paths. "This close, I can smell your deceit—it clings to you like a second skin." 

Aurelia bit back a retort, recognizing in that moment the yawning chasm dividing them. She wore shadows like armor, hiding flaws in a tapestry of lies crafted over years to protect the fragile shell of innocence beneath. But now, as if pushed to the precipice by the merciless tide surrounding them, those defenses cracked and fissured. The poison within began to bleed through, infecting everything.

"My deceit...you wear it better than anyone, Prince Blindfold," she managed, words strained out between gritted teeth as they rounded a corner—stone walls closing in oppressive confines. "Your precious 'trust' has made you blind to the darkness within yourself. How conveniently oblivious you remain to the viper curled in your heart, waiting to strike whenever its master commands."

Anguish twisted his features, lips curling in a snarl. "Silence! You speak too freely with your venom-tongue—"

"Yes! Exactly!" Aurelia cut across his tirade, desperation lending force to each syllable. Her hands clenched in futile protests as his fingers dug punishing lines into her hips through torn silk. "You crave this power, don't you? The ability to control, dominate even those closest to you. Why else pursue me, body and soul, despite the crimes I've committed?" Her nails scraped along the edge of his cloak, seeking anchor in flesh and steel. "Admit it, Caelum—the darkness consumes you just as it does Arkon, gnawing at your very essence until nothing remains but bloodlust, chaos..."

A strangled choke sounded from his throat, a guttural roar cutting off her accusations before they could land the final blow. In response, his arms cinched agonizingly tight, crushing the breath from her gasping lips as his face buried violently into the curve of her shoulder. For a suspended heartbeat, the world held its breath as primal malevolence thrummed beneath his skin, threatening to shatter the last vestiges of control.

Then, just as rapidly, control snapped back into place like a taut bowstring. Caelum yanked away, leaving Aurelia gasping and swaying on trembling legs while frigid night air caressed the sweat-slick expanse of her exposed skin. His chest rose and fell in harsh heaves, eyes burning with feral intensity behind their mask of black silk.

"Now we proceed with the rite—" he pronounced coolly, though the underlying cadence throbbed with a deadly rhythm.

Her vision narrowed to a tunnel of darkness framing the looming silhouette of the execution chamber. Arkon hulked in shadow, awaiting its due as sacred rituals played out according to ancient lore.

✦ ✦ ✦

Aurelia's bare feet scuffled against cold flagstones as she stumbled forward into the chill embrace of the execution chamber, limbs still quivering from Caelum's ruthless grip. The cavernous space felt suffocating, heavy with anticipation and the fetid tang of death. Twin lanterns cast dancing shadows along the basalt walls, each flame reflecting crimson hues in the polished obsidian blade cradled in Caelum's hands. He towered behind her, a silent sentinel cloaked in night-shrouded mystery.

With a subtle twitch of his masked jaw, he urged her onward. "Towards the center, where Arkon awaits its fate." Each syllable resonated with a deep, ominous authority that left no room for defiance. Aurelia's spine stiffened, pride warring with prudence as the heavy hem of her velvet cloak dragged across the stones. She drew near the altar, where the massive serpent lay coiled, muscles tensed and eyes unblinking in the dim light. Its forked tongue flickered out to taste the dank air, a mesmerizing dance woven within seconds.

An electric charge seemed to prick the surface of her skin—the acrid odor of musk and blood clinging thickly to the beast, mingling with the heady allure of something ancient and forbidden. Bile rose in the back of her throat as she glanced at Caelum's unyielding form. Could even a god's blessing taint the corruption festering inside him?

She faltered, and his boots sounded close behind her with deliberate tread. "Do not dally," was all he said, though it echoed far louder than mere words might suggest. With a hand on her elbow, he guided her to stand beside the coils of Arkon, ensuring their thighs touched, hipbones grazing. Cold stone pressed against her lower back, making the small of her spine ache. Every muscle tightened in awareness, heart pounding frantically beneath her rib cage. This proximity was wrong—too intimate for mere acquaintances, yet too formal for any dalliance born of desire.

Caelum stepped around to confront her, the obsidian dagger glinting between their bodies. The edge kissed the slope of her breastbone, a chilling counterpoint to the feverish heat of his gaze piercing the veil of his mask. Aurelia's mouth went dry, pulse escalating to a hectic rhythm in a desperate bid for oxygen. 

"Now," he intoned, the single word thick with portent. 

With a sharp movement, he plunged the daggers crosswise into the serpent's skull. Arkon shuddered violently once, twice, then slumped limply at their feet. Twin rivulets of dark ichor seeped from the punctures, pooling on the slate-gray floor beneath them. Guttural wet hacking sounds filled the heavy air as life drained quickly from the beast in mewling, death throes echoed by the faint ringing of metal on bone.

Breathing ragged, Aurelia jerked away, nearly tripping over her own fleeing footfalls in her haste. But Caelum's grip on her wrist remained implacable, halting her flight across the sacrificial platform. She gasped aloud at the sudden restraint, wrenching her rebellious gaze upwards to meet the smoldering heat in his obsidian orbs.

"Stay," he commanded, low and merciless. "This next part requires your full participation, if you expect to claim victory."  

His other hand reached up to hook beneath her chin, angling her face towards him with a ruthless pressure that made her teeth grind together.

He forced her jaw open with cold precision, slipping past her resistant lips to exhale a warm breath that ghosted along her tongue before withdrawing. A shiver rippled down Aurelia's spine despite herself, the brief contact igniting a hungry hunger that had no business being there amidst screams and bloodletting.

"Now listen carefully, because when I speak again, it will be only twice more." Caelum's voice sliced through the eerie stillness like a scythe.

Aurelia swallowed hard, throat constricting around the lump of fright and something else - fascination? - as she fixated on the way his chest rose and fell with measured inhales against hers.

"The serpent's venom holds power," he continued, dark eyes boring into hers unrelenting, "but not the kind that can sate a goddess' thirst. That requires something far rarer...and something we both possess, courtesy of our divine benefactors. You see," – his thumb pressed downward into her collarbone, applying gentle pressure until she was looking distinctly up at him instead of ahead – "Arkon bore a token within him; a vial of nectar drawn straight from Nyx's own womb. It's the only antidote potent enough to restore equilibrium between light and darkness, humanity and divinity. And you, my dear, hold the key to unlocking it."

Aurelia blanched, shock and revulsion suffusing her system simultaneously. She wrenched her head back out of his grasp, stumbling forward on shaky legs, the vial momentarily flashing before her bulging eyes. Her mind recoiled from the implications even as her body instinctively reacted to another threat creeping closer...

"Nyx," she breathed, horror lacing her tone as comprehension dawned. The celestial goddess infamous for manipulating mortal politics like mere pawns was clearly pulling strings behind the scenes yet again. But why would she choose Aurelia as her unlikely puppet?

Before she could ponder further, the floor beneath them gave a violent lurch, throwing the pair off balance. Arkon, it seemed, hadn't quite finished expiring.

Terror clawed up Aurelia's ribs as the chamber began shuddering and convulsing all around them, the ground bucking wildly. Dust clouds choked the air, the acrid scent of smoke stinging her lungs. Groping blindly, her fingers closed on steel, seeking stability amid the chaos.

"Caa-eluuuum!" she screamed over the thunderous din, desperation coloring her voice as she fumbled desperately for purchase. Her panicked gaze darted around frantically, homing in on the faint outlines of steps cut into the wall just before panic overwhelmed what little reason remained. One desperate leap later, her fingers found purchase on rough stone.

With a gasping cry that blended equal parts fear and exertion, Aurelia hauled herself upward, claws scrabbling inelegantly for footholds as she clambered precipitously toward refuge above.

Behind her, roars of agonized rage and tortured earth rumbled through the chamber, mingling discordantly with the frantic drumbeat of her own heart. Crouched upon the precipice of disaster itself, sweat streaming down her face, she dared one cautious look back—

Only to spy the silhouette of a figure emerging from that infernal storm, striding purposefully through the miasma toward her as calmly as any Sunday stroller promenading the royal gardens. Unfussy black robes billowed out behind him, an ebony shadow stretching forth as if grasping hungrily at all available space.
