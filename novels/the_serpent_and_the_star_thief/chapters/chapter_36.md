# Chapter 36: Chapter 36

His teeth grazed the tender lobe of her ear, a searing brand burned into her consciousness as his breath whispered sinfully against her skin. Aurelia's senses recoiled, even as her body betrayed her, arching into the cruel delight Caelum so sadistically dished out. The world reduced to only him—his touch, his scent, the intoxicating thrum of power that radiated from him like the pulse of a living entity.

Her own heart threatened to burst free from her chest, drumming a furious tattoo against her ribs as she struggled to rein in the inferno that now consumed her entirely. Panic warred with reckless abandon inside her—a maelstrom of emotions that crashed about her mind with the ferocity of a stormy sea.

Through the whirlwind, a glimmer of sanity managed to surface. Aurelia seized upon it, teeth gritted, a fierce inner light burning brighter still behind her eyes. With shaking resolve, she shoved violently against Caelum's formidable form, forcing herself half-awake, half-asleep against the cruel unyieldingness of stone.

He hissed in response, fingers tightening around her wrist until bones ached beneath her fragile, trembling skin. The pain merely served to heighten the tumult raging within her—each searing pinpoint blossoming into molten fire, sweeping her heedlessly onward toward the precipice of total surrender.

A mewling whimper escaped her throat and Aurelia felt her gaze snap wildly about the narrow confines of their cramped hiding place, searching desperately for any means of escape. Her eyes landed once more upon the ornate dagger hilt peeking from its sheath, barely concealed by the folds of her tattered gown.

The knife represented the last shred of autonomy remaining—that tenuous lifeline between her and the abyss. Aurelia's vision zeroed in mercilessly on the weapon, craving nothing more than to plunge its blade deep into Caelum's stubborn, infuriating heart.

But her hand hung suspended, arrested midway to its goal, the fragile thread connecting willpower to action snapping irrevocably beneath the tidal waves of molten rage and lust coursing through her veins. Aurelia watched, detached and horrified, as her fingers curled instead around the vambrace encasing Caelum's arm—the cool metal a stark reminder of his iron dominance over this fragile vessel of skin and bone he had claimed as his own.

Aurelia's traitorous palm flattened against the unyielding armor plating as Caelum maneuvered them bodily out from beneath the ruins, his unbroken form radiating an aura of implacable certainty. Through the turmoil battering her psyche, a wry insight struck: even wounded and shackled alongside her tormentor, Aurelia found herself drawn inexorably, zombie-like, back into the orbit of that all-conquering star.

Each step strained the fine threads reinforcing her bonds—a silent ticking countdown, with no escape hatch but annihilation. As they ventured deeper into the labyrinthine catacombs, the caverns gradually transformed around them—smooth obsidian walls giving way to rough-hewn pillars and arched ceilings adorned with cryptic glyphs that pulsed eerily crimson in the flickering torchlight. 

The air grew heavy with the scent of decay intermingling with an unsettling sweetness, an aroma both familiar and repellent to Aurelia's sensitive nostrils. "Where are we going?" she demanded, though a sinking knowledge belied the question even before it left her lips.

Caelum's reply came in the form of sharp, purposeful strides that ate up the distance, his breaths visible in the chill air and dripping like icicles down the pale column of his throat. "Your precious 'Star Thief' awaits," he murmured finally, his lips curling ironically. "Ready to atone, perhaps?"

Aurelia's answer was a low, strangled choke. Her mind reeled at the thought of facing Drusus again, only this time not as defiant conqueror, but cowering supplicant alongside her jailer. Emasculation laid bare for all eternity—it was almost more intolerable than death itself.

Yet even amidst such crippling despair, her treacherous body refused to surrender the tingles of anticipation that sparked each time Caelum touched her, however briefly. As if sensing the rebellious yearnings roiling beneath her surface, his grip around her wrist tightened further, cold steel biting into soft flesh until beads of blood seeped from newly abraded skin.

"You can't have it both ways, Vex," he spat derisively, jostling her stumbling steps closer to the pulsing heart of the underground complex. "No quarter ever granted, no mercy to be expected in return." His tone dripped malevolent satisfaction, eager to prod and provoke the tempest within her—perhaps goading her towards some catastrophic reckoning that would prove his dominance unequivocally once and for all.

Through gritted teeth, Aurelia hurled her hatred back at him like a poisoned dagger meant to slay and scar. "Mercy? You've never exhibited a single whit of empathy, you soulless monster. Only twisted, base hunger and a thirst for power that consumes everything in your path!" 

With ruthless deliberateness, Caelum brought them to halt against a towering block of black granite, polished to a mirrorlike sheen by the torchflame's dance across its frosty surfaces. An ancient bas-relief depicted a ghastly cycle of birth, suffering, and transcendence carved into the central expanse—a macabre reminder that, like worms on this altar, even immortals were subject to fate’s capricious whims.

Without so much as a glance back, Príncipe de la Noche drew forth a key and fitted it into a recessed lock discreetly camouflaged within the stone. With a subtle click followed by a low hiss of released air, a hidden door swung inward to reveal the antechamber leading into the inner sanctum of Mad Drusus.

As the secret door creaked open, releasing a whisper of chilled air heavy with anticipation, Aurelia's hackles rose in tandem with the prickling sensation along her spine. This couldn't mean what she feared it did—but God help her if her treacherous heart was already half-convinced that Caelum intended to drag their vile game deep enough into the mysterious bowels of this forsaken necropolis to damn both their souls irrevocably.

Drawing himself up to loom over her rigid form, unblinking eyes glinting cruelly behind the folds of his blindfold, he gestured forward in silent command. Aurelia clenched her jaw and resisted the urge to spit defiance in his face, knowing full well it wouldn't deter him—he thrived on her wrath, craved every drop of poison she could spare.

With measured strides, they entered the dimly lit chamber beyond, each step sending eerie echoes reverberating off cold stone walls adorned with esoteric symbols and arcane runic patterns flickering yellow under the sputtering torchlight. A thick, pungent miasma hung heavy in the stagnant air, tinged with the reek of forgotten sacrifices and decay, as if the very room itself had absorbed centuries of death and despair.

Príncipe de la Noche halted at the farthest edge of the sanctum, one arm outstretched to steady Aurelia against a nearby pedestal just as she began to sway, her knees threatening to buckle under the weight of her spiraling dread. Heat suffused her cheeks, sweat beading upon her clammy brow even as an icy dread coursed through her veins.

"Priya ke pasand ki jagah nahi hai yahan," he whispered huskily into her ear—the guttural timbre of his words barely audible above the cloying drone of wings and the distant howl of some tortured creature. "Tujhe kabhi na bhulungi." Never forget you.

Her gorge rose, yet she stood rooted before him, powerless to flee this crucible of dark temptation where the maniacal puppeteer held the strings—and with each passing moment, coiled tighter around his fingers. It shouldn't feel so... seductive. So tantalizingly dangerous to surrender to the inferno that threatened to incinerate every last shred of control, but damn her cursed allure to him! Even knowing it spelled ruin, Aurelia found herself aching to indulge in the illicit thrill, craving this forbidden dance with a daemon who relished her agony like fine wine.

"Achha hothon pe kabhi to khilnay chahiye." Sometimes even monsters deserve a taste of sweetness. The tender scrape of his knuckles against her cheekbone had shockwaves crashing through her system like a tidal wave, stealing her breath in a gasp as liquid fire flooded her veins and drowned bitter protestations that refused to surface.

"No..." Aurelia whimpered, though whether in rebellion or reluctant acquiescence, she couldn't have said. Not even as Príncipe de la Noche's large hand cupped the fragile column of her throat, thumb brushing gently back and forth to expose the throbbing pulse beneath fair skin. "I won't be your—your... pawn anymore."

"Oh, no? Then why do I smell your desire intermingled with the fear?" He leaned closer, breath hot and humid against her earlobe. "It clouds my senses, masks any pretense of revulsion and leaves me wanting more."

The scent of smoldering ash hung heavy in the air between them as Príncipe de la Noche released Aurelia's abused flesh from his grasp. Her throat convulsed, raw and parched from unmoaned cries, the bruising imprint of his fingers still seared into plump skin. With halting breaths, she struggled to process the maelstrom of sensations that had raged through her during their depraved tango – each caress, every husky whisper weaving a spell of dizzying, sweet submission.

Darkness still shrouded Aurelia's vision, a void where judgment and restraint once resided now obliterated by the ravaging power of her desires. Her resolve had crumbled, pulverized under the relentless onslaught of his touch until naught remained but a pitiful plea for more. And more she would receive – for now Príncipe de la Noche reveled in her defeat, his own primal hunger eclipsed only by sadistic glee at witnessing her fall.

"Aurelia..." His voice wrapped around her like a cloak, the velvety timbre achingly familiar despite all they had suffered together. One step forward and he could engulf her entirely within his arms, trap her close enough that she'd need merely tip her chin upward for his lips to claim hers again. The thought sent fresh flames racing along her nerve endings, making her tremble and cling to tattered pride all the same.

"Stay," came a guttural command from the shadows, halting Aurelia mid-breath. Príncipe de la Noche froze behind her, eyes narrowed as they tracked the newcomer's approach. Even before sight confirmed it, the electric thrum of eldritch energy that preceded Zoriel rendered identification unnecessary.

"Well." Breath hissed out between gleaming fangs, her golden gaze cold as winter frost and piercing as shattered glass. "What a surprise. Enjoying a momentary reprieve from tormenting innocent subjects, I see." Zoriel stepped closer, looming over Aurelia like an implacable specter while disdain dripped from every syllable. "And I thought you had better things to occupy your nightstalker attention."

"Oh, I've been quite occupied, sister star thief." The darkness seemed to writhe and twist about Prince Caelum as malevolent power surged through him; when he spoke next, his voice emerged low and laced with malice. "In fact, we were just discussing ways to make your presence... even more interesting."
