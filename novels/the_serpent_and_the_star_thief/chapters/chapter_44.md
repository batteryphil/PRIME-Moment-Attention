# Chapter 44: Chapter 44

Archon Xandros paused just shy of touching distance, that wickedly knowing smile playing about his cruel lips like a macabre jest. He stood there in smug dominance, the air itself seeming to thicken and vibrate with antagonistic energy between him, Aurelia, and Caelum. One gloved finger traced along the sharp edge of a curved dagger sheathed at his hip before languidly trailing upward to stroke along the jawline Caelum had been born with.

"You've chosen a precarious moment to entertain, Blind Prince," Xandros purred, the sound sending shivers dancing from nape to groin along both captives as it caressed the ears. "I hope your paramour appreciates this moment of carnal pause." With no further warning, he lunged, snatching Aurelia from behind Caelum's shield and slamming her against the wall. Her shriek harmonized with Caelum's enraged growl. 

Cold steel pressed hotly against her throat, and Xandros dragged back her head with brutal force, cutting off anything Aurelia might attempt by way of desperate retort or escape. She gulped frantically around the pressure of blade, staring wildly up into the Archon's triumphant face now mere inches from hers. His features shifted eerily close to Caelum's—a demonic mirror image—and yet his eyes burned with an unsettling different shade of madness.

"Foolish girl," Xandros hissed, the obsidian depth of his gaze hypnotic and terrifying turned upon her trembling form. "Did you truly believe I'd allow the Blind Executioner's sloppy seconds to claim victory? After all the sacrifices..." His grip tightened. The steel bit deeper into skin, drawing threads of crimson down Aurelia's vulnerable neck to soak into her golden locks. "I have other uses for your... particular talents," he continued, voice dripping with sadistic promise as he drank in the sight of her paled cheeks flushed with terror and revulsion.

"XANDROS!" Caelum bellowed, charging forward into the fray with desperate resolve even as dread clutched his innards, every fiber of training screaming that he was utterly outmatched here. But damned if the blind prince knew how to back down in the face of such vile intimidation or cruelty directed at those he protected. With an explosive burst of speed despite not being fully sighted, Caelum slammed a shoulder into Xandros, aiming to dislodge Aurelia and create some temporary reprieve.

The blow connected hard, and Aurelia tumbled free as Caelum wrestled with his monstrous doppelganger for dominance. Her mind spun—pain-pulverizing shock robbing thoughts of context, her heart jack-hammering against ribs as she scrambled to press herself instead behind Caelum for cover once more, seeking refuge in the rock-solid strength of his unmoving form.

This time, rather than shield her, Caelum's massive frame cocooned hers, sheltering her back as both fighters surged powerfully across the chamber, their struggles echoing violently against the walls and ceiling while chairs overturned and tapestries crumpled beneath frenzied feet. The very air crackled as magic war-torches ignited around them, casting fitful strobes of lurid colors starkly over contorted faces smeared with grime and sweat, gleaming weapons, and rippling muscles straining against fabric as they dueled.

Aurelia squeezed her eyes shut, burying her face against Caelum's broad back to block the chaotic kaleidoscope before them.

Aurelia clung desperately to Prince Caelum's solid warmth as the cataclysmic tempest raged about them. Caelum's body was a fortress shielding her, trembling with barely-leashed fury as his fists pummeled Xandros mercilessly.

"You think the serpent you've courted," Came Xandros' acid sneer, "would ever choose a pathetic cripple like you over me?"

The prince threw back his head in agony-filled snarls, black hair whipping wildly about his skull. His hands lacerated Xandros' skin in a blur of daggers and razors until Xandros recoiled, clutching bloody gashes.

"Not cripp— AHH!" Caelum roared, suddenly convulsing violently as some unseen force clawed up his side towards his breastbone, making him buckle forward. Aurelia yelped as her cheek bruised painfully against his back by the brutal shift; her flailing fingers scrabbled for purchase on his armor-clad chest as he staggered awkwardly.

Xandros seethed in triumph, blood-spattered robes fluttering wildly as his voice rose to maniacal pitches. Around them, the conflagration of eldritch flames leaping across the room's surfaces pulsed in eerie synchronicity with his erratic laughter.

"Yes! Ahh yes, look what your precious Vespera does, pet! She tears your soul apart for defying her command!"

Caelum keened, a sound raw with anguish and helpless rage, as icy tendrils stole over his torso, sucking all semblance of warmth from his body. He shuddered brutally, rigidening and falling to one knee as invisible bonds tightened painfully against his diaphragm and shoulders. Forced to its knees beside him, Aurelia found her gaze riveted to his profile etched with unutterable pain...and her traitorous pulse raced in fierce delight at the sheer, wrenching vulnerability painted starkly across his gaunt features.

"No..." she whispered to the darkness surrounding them, transfixed. "No, this ends NOW..."

Rhythmic thunder exploded from her direction—the unmistakable sound of twin daggers sliding from their braced holds in her palms. In that frozen instant before chaos erupted anew across the gore-stained chamber floor, two glittering silver blades pierced straight through the leather-bound journal clutched in Xandros' trembling hand.

Inky black pages sprayed out like shredded ribbons as she wrenched the daggers free with ruthless ease, metal scraping against bone. A shriek tore from his lips, but it quickly soured into guttural curses as crimson gushed between his fingers to drip heedlessly onto the stones below.

Behind her, she felt Caelum seize upright, quivering hands flying to claw away the crushing eldritch force writhing against his flesh—only to cry out again in dismay as spectral wisps wriggled past his defensive wards to weave themselves sinuously around her trembling, blade-handled upper arms like living chains. 

He whirled roughly, seizing hold of Aurelia's wrists with clammy yet commanding strength to stop her from stabbing blindly through the darkling fog once more. Through gritted teeth, he barked an imperative order she would recognize anywhere—"Steady, viper! Target those ethereal serpents choking you before you slice yourself open!"

Though still locked in each other's gazes, neither party noticed the sinister figure in the depths of the shadows subtly weaving between statues and pillar silhouettes with uncanny stealth.

Pulsating violet light seared into sensitive retinas even as Aurelia ripped her burning gaze from that eerie, otherworldly beauty materializing behind Xandros. Ethanol-tightened stomach coiled in response to the sorcerous spectacle—the sheer audacity of a rival stealing one's most prized discovery had struck a nerve deep within her predatory soul, firing off adrenaline to mix with the acrid tang of alchemical acid already churning up her throat.

But Caelum's harsh interruption brutally yanked her focus back to the present, snapping the taut string of concentration connecting body and mind. Her muscles seized as if turned to solid mercury; silver daggers slipped clumsily from numb, slick fingers to clatter loudly on the flags at their feet—a sound that seemed to ricochet off the vaulted ceilings, reverberate down walls slick with blood, and whisper a dirge for the life flickering dimly in Xandros' glassy orbs.

In the stunned silence that followed, cold rationality struggled for dominance over visceral reaction, but there was no denying the way Aurelia's chest continued to heave with ragged breaths as she felt, rather than saw, the ghost-white face of her prince peering intently beneath its woven concealment. Felt too his gloved thumbs kneading the delicate bones of her wrists where he gripped so firmly, sending sparks dancing along her overheated skin despite the chill seeping through her soaked sleeve.

The steady, imperious tone sent a shiver down her spine—the command thrumming through his voice sounded almost... fond? It twisted strangely in her chest, igniting a spark of something hot and vulnerable that she desperately wanted to snuff out by default. 'He trusts me,' some distant, rational part whispered. She glanced sideways to see tears glazing Xandros' fever-addled eyes, the wet streaks tracking his dirty cheeks impossibly poignant amidst the carnage strewn about them both. Aurelia bit back a whimper, shoulders hunching beneath Caelum's hold.

"Master?" Xandros rasped, his usually melodious voice reduced to a cracked, petulant wail edged with desperation. "I don't wanna... I'm scared..." Crimson bubbled audibly from the corner of his torn lip, drooling down chin and neck in morbid rivulets. She could practically taste the copper-metallic iron on her tongue.

Beneath a thin veneer of cold calculation, another war raged behind Aurelia's eyes. Part of her yearned to be strong, unyielding, to prove she wasn't fazed by any amount of death or darkness this forsaken world flung at her. But another hungered for comfort, for shelter from the storm gathering within these labyrinthine halls and threatening to crush her very being beneath its weight. In that moment of breathtaking loneliness, none seemed clearer than one man—a tall, imposing silhouette framed by the jagged mouth of an arched doorway as a distant bell tolled mournfully in the cathedral above.

With a silent, anguished moan, Aurelia shoved aside years of conditioning, layers of steel-reinforced habituation that always left her oddly unsatisfied despite achieving peak performance. Steeling herself against exhaustion, cold dread sinking heavily in her belly that weighed almost as much as the twin wounds crisscrossing her torso, she instead focused intently on the phantom warmth generated by Caelum's hold. His palm drifted up to brush hair wisped away from her cheek, fingers grazing her temple in maddeningly gentle strokes meant solely for soothing, not seduction, as if to assuage her fears.

The warm press of Caelum's fingertips softened Aurelia's gaze, drawing her attention into the curve of his palm like a moth to a flame. Time lost meaning as those slow, exquisite caresses replaced every other sensation—pounding heart, shallow gasps, heavy thighs trembling with the urge to surrender, to give in. 

But just as swiftly, fragile trust shattered as another sound pierced the hushed chamber—an agonized shriek tearing through the air, abruptly cutting the fragile spell that had bonded them. The prince froze, brows drawn into a sharp V, as a frantic patter echoed closer, growing louder before skidding to a stop before their bench.

"Holy Mother," someone breathed, voice shaking like autumn leaves. "Look what they did... "

Slowly, Aurelia levered herself upright on shaky legs, pain hammering through her midsection with each clumsy movement until raw agony blurred vision and dulled thought. But even through the haze, a horrifying image took shape—misshapen body crumpled against the far wall where the scream originated, blood painting the tile floor an obscene crimson pool.

Golden light spilled inward as the cathedral doors lurched open, and Prince Orion strode in, expression ashen. A small crowd clustered behind him: scholars in rumpled robes, priests clutching intricately carved prayer bones, all cast anxious glances between the grisly tableau and the newly arisen pair. 

"This isn't possible," Orion declared hoarsely, gaze riveting on the mutilated form at their feet. "Vasilisa Blackwood, a woman of unparalleled cunning and power, has been reduced to...this."

Pain flared like a gypsy fire through Aurelia's chest as she shifted to face her brother—a subtle tightening of muscles perhaps, but Orion's eyes widened, alarm flashing across his countenance as he recoiled involuntarily.

"Dear gods," he whispered, face blanching anew. "Aurelia—your wounds! How can you..."

No words mattered anymore. Fractured thoughts swirled to oblivion beneath a red haze of rage tempered only by searing grief. Bitter bile scorched the back of Aurelia's throat as she lunged for her broken sister, faltering steps eating at her own flesh like acid, yet driven onward by primitive maternal need.

"Vasilisa!" she wept, collapsing beside the corpselike figure. Crimson stained Aurelia's tears as desperate fingers combed matted black hair, searching for the spark of life amidst the viscera spilling out onto the stones.

Yet amidst the horror, something strange stirred deep within her chest—a sense alien and yet not unfamiliar, echoing eerily the pulse of the celestial machinery spinning unseen above. As if awakening from centuries-long slumber, ancient magic kindled to life. Aurelia felt it thrumming through her veins like a living inferno, each ragged breath exhaling scorching tendris around her.

And then, the final, shattering realization: Aurelia was no ordinary viperess. Whatever horrors Kira and Malakai sought would merely speed this transformation along, fueling her ascension toward powers beyond mortal comprehension.

As the world narrowed to nothingness around her—sound fading, light dimming, senses retreating into some primal core—"Sister" became a redundant term in Aurelia Vex's mind. One serpent soul would soon swallow the rest.

This new goddess would rise from the ashes—the Serpent Empress—reborn from death itself. And her first act of dominion would be ushered forth with Prince Corvin's dying kiss...
