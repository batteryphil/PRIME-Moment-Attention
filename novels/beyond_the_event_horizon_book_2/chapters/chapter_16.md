# Chapter 16: Bruises and Starlight

Leo stumbled against his chair's handholds, the Starlight Marauder's sudden cast-off almost sending him careening into the bulkhead. Stars wheeled madly outside the fisheye lens of the main screen as the pirate frigate surged back into motion, its thrusters screaming with renewed fury. The faint tang of ozone and burning metal stung Leonardo's nostrils. Beside him, Astrid gripped the control yoke firmly, knuckles white as she fought to stabilize their unpredictable course through the wreckage-strewn, lawless reaches of the Kuiper Belt.

"We're clear of the debris field," Astrid managed to say over the howling roar of retrofiring engines, "but this ride isn't over yet."

Her voice seemed far away to Leo, swallowed up by the reverberating thrum of tortured metal screaming through the ship's damaged superstructure, threatening to overload the straining stress bearings at any moment. Cold sweat pooled between his shoulder blades and trickled down the slope of his ribs although he felt nothing, paralyzed by terror in the zero-G crucible of their plummeting flight.

Mercifully, the gale of sound began to subside as the main drives throttled back to conserve fuel, still throwing out radiant heat that painted their faces in sickly shades of crimson. Through half-lidded eyes, Leo saw his own reflection in the dimmed cabin lights, cheekbones striking and eyes wide with unshed panic behind smudged goggles. He forced himself to concentrate, reaching for the comms button with numb fingers.

"Astrid—"

A klaxon pierced the air, cutting him off, casting eerie shadows across the scarred deckplates as emergency alerts splashed across every available display. Laser tachyon pulses crackled the very ether itself above their heads and a chittering swarm of missiles arced into existence, hungry seekers homing on the pirate frigate's hot engines and exposed mass.

The ship shook violently, responding reflexively to the incoming barrage, thrusters firing in a wild pattern as it dodged erratically through the swirling dust clouds of an ancient comet's shattered nucleus. Alarms shrieked and popped, spitting sparks as systems went offline with a dying gasp.

Leo scrabbled backward, slamming against the curved wall as a near miss sent them slewing into a spin, threatening to toss him limb-over-limb until Astrid dragged the emergency harness over both their heads, clips snapping shut with final conviction.

They hung there in brutal darkness, gasping for breath as the ship's inertial dampeners whined in overwhelmed distress. Then, agonizingly slow, the Starlight Marauder came back online, stabilizers kicking in to halt the mad pirouette. Leo sagged against the constraints, vision blackening at the edges as his body struggled to reconcile the universe tilting wildly about it with the reassuring normality of the world below the horizon line.

Gradually the klaxons ceased their piercing wail, replaced by a soft hiss of equalizing pressure as sealed compartments purged their pockets of toxic gases. Warm illumination spilled out across the cluttered bridge - the first rays of sunset painting the broken hull, stark silhouettes of twisted girders and flickering plasma torches against the fiery glow creeping up from the atmosphere below, igniting cirrus clouds into rippling banners of rose and ultramarine to frame the blazing celestial pyre that had birthed the Earth...

The pair clung to each other, battered but unbroken, bodies slick with sweat and the acrid tang of ozone where they hung tethered against the wall.

✦ ✦ ✦

The battered, angular faceplate of Lieutenant Commander Leo Mercer cracked open with a hiss of equalization, revealing bloodshot eyes glazed with fatigue beneath sweat-damp fringe of bangs. Harsh crimson light flooded the cramped bridge, mingling with faint sulfurous reek of oxidized hydrazine, scorched polymer decks, and the lingering musk of Vex's heated presence wedged close beside him.

"Sundown already," he croaked, throat abraded raw from screaming into his mask during the ship's catastrophic wobble. "I was hoping we'd get some daylight salvage operations done before..."

His gaze shifted to Astrid, tracing the livid cuts and bruises shadowing her cheekbone, jawline, and the side of her neck where she'd been belted into place against the tossing deck. Stubble rash marked a fresh trail down her chin where her helmet hadn't seated properly under the chaos of their violent spinning. A jagged gash above her left eyebrow stood out as freshly treated stitches, still puffy and streaked red. She met his glance, eyes flinty beneath sooty lashes after hours spent smothered in a helmet, expression unreadable behind a dusting of grime.

"We've got a lousy hole blown in our starboard flank," Astrid said flatly. "Not that you haven't noticed." Her tone was a sarcastic dare, voice gritty from screamed obscenities into her comms rig as alarm klaxons wailed deafening fury. 

"Damned right I saw it," Leo snapped back. "When every g-force tries to rip my arms out of their sockets and shitstorm itself all around us!" He jabbed a finger toward a grim maw of splintered alloy and charred wiring gaping in the bulkhead opposite them, rent open like ravaged meat, venting depressurized air in pinging rivulets to the gravity-warped tangle of debris-strewn decks far below. "You nearly got me killed! Again!"

Astrid barked a harsh laugh, brittle-edged. "You're not exactly a spring chicken yourself, hotshot. And hey, if I hadn't gotten my act together, you would be dead dead-dead - fried like bug meat in microwaves on Pluto's moon instead of bruised and moderately alive right now."

She jerked a rough thumb toward the hulking breach framing the viewscreen, which itself glowed sickly emerald from the searing heat and ionized plasmas still licking at exposed electrical conduits. "This? My problem. You should focus on patching your precious fighter."

"If only it were as simple as slapping a Band-Aid over a hole this size..." He let out a choked sigh that rustled the thinning wisps of hair curling from beneath his goggles, hands gripping the edge of the console rigidly enough to whiten his knuckles. "We need materials, tools, spare parts. Shit, the hydroponics bay's gone too, so who knows how fast we'll run out of breathable air once the scrubbers start playing catch-up?"

Leo swiveled his head to stare out the viewscreen, lip curled in revulsion at the sight of smoking hull plates melted and buckled beyond recognition. Fragments of pulverized structure adrift in vacuum looked like errant diamonds against the rich crimsons and golds of the setting nebula - hellish aurorae born from scalding lightning arcing along the disrupted energy grids stretching across what remained of their hull. Injured people drifting lifelessly within the maelstrom made the gruesome spectacle all too real.

✦ ✦ ✦

Commander Leo Mercer's gaze remained transfixed on the apocalyptic vista dominating the shattered viewport, where the Starlight Marauder's violated remains fluttered amid a gauzy dance of stellar auroras. His mind reeled at the calamitous extent of the damage – all the more staggering an affront given the corsair vessel's resilient design.

With a slow, deliberate blink, he disconnected himself from the tactical display embedded in his flight suit and floated free, the faint tug of artificial gravity barely perceptible within the ship's core. Stubble-bristled jaw clenched tight beneath his cowl, Leo propelled himself towards Vex with slow, powerful strokes of his arms. 

The pirate captain watched him approach through a haze of fatigue and agony, her emerald eyes red-rimmed and dark crescents etched below them by shadowy bruises. Tattered strands of platinum hair clung damply to her face, mingling with streaks of graphite residue from the superheated blast that had torn open their hull. Crimson droplets of blood welled anew from a jagged nick above her left eyebrow, trickling down a cheekbone hollowed by pain and exhaustion.

Leo arrived before her, reaching out to gently cup a pale hand around the bleeding cut. Vex flinched instinctively at the contact, then exhaled a tremulous breath as she met his concerned gaze. Silence stretched between them as Leo tenderly dabbed at the wound with the fabric of his flight suit, smearing the crimson stains into the gray fabric.

Neither captain spoke. The sheer devastation surrounding them had rendered words inadequate, every breath tasting of dust and smoke and acrid fear. Finally, Leo eased back, releasing his grip on Vex's wrist. A wry half-smile tugged at the corner of his mouth as he gazed upon her with something akin to tenderness – though the bruise-darkening crescents framing those captivating eyes suggested that look might be tinged with the same avarice for victory that usually illuminated the depths of her own irises.

"This isn't over," Leo said softly, his voice carrying the gravity of unspoken resolve. "We've got damaged ship, wounded crew, but as long as there's air in our tanks and charge in our drives... we haven't lost yet."

A slow nod met his statement, Vex's ruby lips parting to utter a rasping "Agreed." Then, as if the admission conjured some inner defiance, her expression transformed. Eyes flashing alight with an alluring mix of weariness, determination, and feral hunger, Vex lunged inwards, claiming Leo's parted lips with a fervent, bruising kiss.

For one surreal instant, the world ceased to exist outside the intimate pressure of their mouth-to-mouth collision – two survivors locked together in a momentary union of desperation and raw, instinctual craving. Then Vex abruptly broke away with an audible gasp, leaving Leo stunned and grappling to reconcile the sudden shift from gentle tenderness to fiery abandon.

"I'd rather perish in the void than have you suffer because of me," Vex stated, voice husky with lingering emotion. Before Leo could respond, she'd already spun away, striding purposefully towards an auxiliary engineering station nestled between banks of instrument panels.

Leo trailed after her, hands bunched into fists at his sides as the aftermath of that electric kiss echoed through his veins like supernova aftershocks. "Dammit, Vex –"

"Not now," she cut him off without glance, already hacking at a console display with grim efficiency.

✦ ✦ ✦

Vex's fingers flew across the auxiliary engineering panel's holographic keys, hacking into the life support systems with a precision born of countless battles against desperate odds. The staccato beep of override protocols filled the close-quarters compartment, punctuated by sporadic bursts of flickering diagnostic lights that cast an eerie glow on the crewman's haggard features.

"Don't worry—still ninety seconds before atmosphere decompression." Her tone brooked no argument even as the words dripped with acid fatigue, each syllable a manifestation of the monumental stress currently gnawing at her psyche.

Leo's frustration simmered just beneath the surface, his chest heaving with labored breaths as he struggled to reconcile the pirate captain's abrupt about-face following that explosive, heady kiss. His instincts screamed for retaliation against whatever internal torment drove her to this point, but the dire emergency situation commanding their full attention forced a fragile truce between them.

The close quarters only added to the volatile energy crackling in the air, the tight space amplifying every subtle nuance of their interactions. As Vex bent over the console, her raven hair swung forward to partially obscure her profile, the sleek lines of her combat-grade envirosuit accentuating the taut muscles rippling down her back under the luminescent fabrics.

Suddenly, the captain jerked upright, green eyes wide as saucers behind thick-rimmed goggles. "Leo—look!"

He followed her frantic gesture toward the curved viewscreen, eyes rounding in tandem with hers at the otherworldly spectacle unfolding beyond the protective shielding of the Marauder's hull. An impossible confluence of cosmic forces was staging a cataclysmic showdown mere kilometers away—a colossal neutron-star merger whose unfathomable energies would soon reshape entire star systems.

Swirls of scintillating plasma coalesced into churning vortexes that warped light itself, while the telltale crimson haze of exotic matter blooms marked areas where reality crumbled under the overwhelming tidal stresses. Stars within hundreds of parsecs wobbled and pulsed erratically in sympathetic resonance with the binary behemoths at the heart of the clash.

"All sensors on max!" Vex commanded in a voice suddenly devoid of emotion, even as the mesmerizing sight triggered primal responses within both pirates. "Anything hinting at hostiles should be flagged immediately."

They had scant minutes to prepare for what this astronomical coup de grâce might unleash upon the cosmos—and possibly, unsuspecting vessels like theirs caught in its firing line. Even amidst the turmoil of the universe bending to nature's unrelenting will, the shared weight of mutual survival hung palpably in the air, forcing unwanted parallels between their desperate orbits and the doomed dance of titanic celestial bodies visible before them.
